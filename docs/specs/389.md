# Issue #389: Money position card — one signed chain ending in SAFE TO SPEND

**Auditing branch:** `factory/389`

**Depends on:** PNL-1 (SafeToSpend and Projection types)

## Problem

The money position card (`src/app/jobs/money-position-client.tsx`) currently renders "SET ASIDE" (VAT) and "WHAT'S LEFT" as two disconnected sections. A trade glancing at the card to decide whether they can afford an expense sees "What's left" labeled as "Money collected, minus costs you've paid" — which reads like a final figure but is actually gross of VAT they owe HMRC and gross of the motko fee. The card's job is to be the one place where that answer is safe to take, but today a VAT-registered trade over-reads their true position by the VAT amount they need to set aside.

The motko fee does not appear on the card at all. VAT appears twice in incompatible roles: once as an amount to set aside, and again implicitly in "What's left" (which has no VAT deduction in its calculation, verified at `money-position-actions.ts:231`). Nothing on the card tells the trade these two numbers interact.

## Scope: what changes

Restructure the money position card to display a single signed arithmetic chain leading to **SAFE TO SPEND**, with the forward-looking projection below it, subordinate:

```
COMING IN  (not counted below)
  Jacob Buckland · £240.00 · 3 days

MONEY IN AND OUT  (money actually received)
  Collected                      £119.04
  Costs paid                      −£0.00
  motko fees                      −£0.00
  VAT to set aside               −£19.84
  ─────────────────────────────────────
  SAFE TO SPEND                   £99.20

  If everything owed lands       £299.20  ▸
```

### Display rules

1. **Every subtraction visible and signed**: minus sign on every deducted row (costs, fees, VAT)
2. **SAFE TO SPEND is the total** of the chain, below a visual rule
3. **"Safe to spend" replaces "What's left"** — retire the string "Money collected, minus costs you've paid" entirely
4. **Section caption** is "money actually received" to distinguish from COMING IN (which is explicitly not counted in the chain)
5. **VAT breakdown becomes a disclosure**: "VAT collected" and "VAT on costs" move behind it; only net VAT-to-set-aside appears on the chain
6. **Estimate disclaimer moves** to the VAT line (inside the disclosure or beneath that row), because VAT is the only estimated term on the chain. It currently sits under the whole section, implying the total is an estimate.
7. **Zero fee renders at £0.00, present** — not gated on `FEE_BILLING_ENABLED`. The fee line must appear even when zero.
8. **Not VAT-registered** → no VAT row at all (not a £0.00 row)

### Projection (forward-looking total)

- Renders one line beneath SAFE TO SPEND, visually subordinate
- Smaller than SAFE TO SPEND (not `size="total"`)
- Label: "If everything owed lands" (not "Total", not "Expected")
- Own working behind a disclosure, in same signed-chain shape
- Uses **net** owed figure (VAT already removed), not gross from COMING IN
- Example: VAT-registered, £240.00 gross owed, £99.20 safe-to-spend → projection is **£299.20** (not £339.20)
- PNL-1 supplies `owedNet` — the owed invoices with VAT already taken out

### What the card does NOT do

The card **renders** the breakdown that PNL-1 **computes**. The only permitted arithmetic at the render boundary is `/100` unit conversion (pence to pounds). The card must not add, subtract, or scale money terms. In particular: do not compute the projection by adding COMING IN to safe-to-spend — that is the spec's §7 error.

## Explicitly out of scope

- Computing the breakdown or projection (PNL-1 owns both)
- The voice "What's left?" answer (PNL-4)
- FEE_BILLING_ENABLED split
- Job-level payout breakdown
- Flat-rate scheme, CIS reverse charge, partial exemption (disclaimer already covers these and moves as-is)
- Fee bands

## Files

### Modified files
- `src/app/jobs/money-position-client.tsx` — restructure to render the signed chain and projection
- `src/components/ui/money.tsx` — accept an optional `data-testid` and pass it to the span

#### Why money.tsx is in scope

Added after review, and on different reasoning than was offered for it.

The acceptance tests read rendered figures back out of the DOM —
`pence("safe-to-spend")` and friends resolve `screen.getByTestId(id).textContent`
— because criterion 1's whole point is that the total must follow from the
numbers actually on screen, not from the fixture that produced them. `Money`
took `{ amount, size?, className? }` and nothing else, so no call site could
label the figure it rendered.

**It is not true that the tests cannot pass otherwise.** They resolve
`textContent`, so wrapping each figure — `<span data-testid="collected"><Money
… /></span>` — satisfies every one of them without touching a shared
component, and stays inside the file list as first written. That option was
available and is the reason this is a judgement rather than a necessity.

The passthrough wins on the merits, not by default. Wrapping puts an extra
element around every money value on a card whose entire subject is its layout —
a signed chain that has to align and total correctly — and adds markup that
exists only for the tests. One optional prop on a leaf presentational component
is smaller, changes no rendering when it is absent, and reads at the call site
as what it is.

So it is declared here, rather than the file list being treated as satisfied by
an argument that the change was unavoidable.

## Edge cases

1. **Nothing collected at all**: Collected £0.00, chain renders, SAFE TO SPEND £0.00. Do not hide the section; an empty chain is a true answer.

2. **Not VAT-registered**: VAT row absent entirely (not a £0.00 row). The estimate disclaimer goes with it since nothing on the chain is an estimate any more. If the projection renders, its disclosure keeps a short note that the fee figure is estimated.

3. **Zero fee**: Fee line renders at £0.00, present (criterion 3)

4. **Negative safe-to-spend**: Render the sign, no clamping (costs + fees exceed collected). No red-alert styling in this ticket; the number does the work.

5. **Projection lower than safe-to-spend**: Possible when unpaid costs exceed what is owed. Render as-is; do not suppress a worse projection.

6. **Long counterparty names in COMING IN**: Unchanged from today's truncate behavior

7. **Disclosures default state**: Both closed. (Do not write the literal `defaultOpen={false}` in comments in this file — test 306 counts occurrences including comments)

8. **Nothing owed and nothing unpaid**: Projection equals safe-to-spend. Render it or omit it, but if it renders, arithmetic must hold (criterion 8)

## Risks

- **Money display**: This changes what the biggest number on the money position card represents. Currently it's "What's left" (gross of VAT and fee); after this change it's "Safe to spend" (net of both). A trade who has internalized the old number will see a smaller one.
- **No migration required** — display-only change
- **No new data storage** — renders what PNL-1 computes
- **Dependency on PNL-1**: Tests will fail with "Cannot find module" for SafeToSpend/Projection types until PNL-1 merges. This is expected and correct.

## Derived criteria

What I understood the human to be asking for:

1. **Replace the disconnected two-section layout** (SET ASIDE + WHAT'S LEFT) with a single signed arithmetic chain where every deduction is visible with a minus sign, leading to SAFE TO SPEND as the total

2. **Make SAFE TO SPEND the biggest number** on the card (size="total"), with the forward-looking projection below it, visually subordinate (smaller)

3. **Render the fee at zero** even when `FEE_BILLING_ENABLED` is false — the fee line must appear, gated only on having any money activity at all

4. **Remove VAT entirely when not registered** (no row at all), but render it as a single net figure (VAT to set aside) when registered, with the breakdown (collected vs on costs) behind a disclosure

5. **Move the estimate disclaimer** from under the whole section to the VAT line specifically, since VAT is the only estimated term

6. **Display the projection using net figures** (VAT already removed) from PNL-1, not by adding gross COMING IN figures

7. **Perform no currency arithmetic in the card** — render what PNL-1 computes; the only permitted operation is `/100` for unit conversion at the display boundary

8. **Handle negative totals and edge cases gracefully**: render negative safe-to-spend with sign, render projections that are worse than current position, show the chain even when collected is £0.00
