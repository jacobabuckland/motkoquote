## Investigation complete

The investigation requested in the issue has been completed and documented in the issue body. Key findings:

**Field-to-column mapping (`main` @ `844e788`):**

| Field label | Section | Written to |
|---|---|---|
| Company name | Company | `contractors.company_name` (real column) |
| Company number | Company | `contractors.business_profile.company_number` (JSONB) |
| Trading name (if different) | Legal & contract details | `contractors.business_profile.trading_name` (JSONB) |
| Business structure | Legal & contract details | `contractors.business_profile.business_structure` (JSONB) |

**Conclusion:** The original premise does not hold. There are **no duplicate fields** — each field appears exactly once in the tree. Company name and Trading name are genuinely different things (registered company name vs trading name), but they may read as the same question to someone filling out the form.

## Problem

The setup form has two fields that may be confusing to users:
- "Company name" appears in the Company section (required)
- "Trading name (if different)" appears in the Legal & contract details section (optional)

While these are genuinely different concepts (registered company name vs the name a business trades under), the form provides no explanation of this distinction. A contractor filling out the form may be unclear about:
- What "trading name (if different)" means
- When they should fill it in vs leaving it blank
- How it differs from the company name they just entered

## Current behavior

The setup form (`/setup`) has two collapsible sections:

**Company section:**
- Companies House search
- Company name field (required) → writes to `contractors.company_name`
- Company number field (optional) → writes to `contractors.business_profile.company_number`
- Validation warnings if entered details don't match Companies House

**Legal & contract details section:**
- Trading name (if different) field (optional) → writes to `contractors.business_profile.trading_name`
- Business structure field (optional) → writes to `contractors.business_profile.business_structure`
- Other required fields (address, phone, email, etc.)
- Helper text: "Used to fill in the contracts you send customers — set once, reused on every job"
- Helper text: "Leave any optional field blank and that clause simply won't appear on your contracts"

## What needs deciding

The investigation confirmed there is no data duplication defect. What remains is a UX decision about whether and how to clarify the distinction between these fields. Options include:

(a) Add helper text below "Trading name (if different)" explaining when to use it  
(b) Change the "Trading name (if different)" label to make the distinction clearer  
(c) Reorder sections or group fields differently  
(d) Keep as-is — the "(if different)" qualifier and existing section structure are sufficient  

This is customer-facing copy on legal/business setup fields, which falls under "Customer-facing legal or contractual copy" on the escalation list.

## DECISION NEEDED

**Question:** Should the setup form clarify the distinction between "Company name" and "Trading name (if different)", and if so, how?

**Context:** 
- The fields are not duplicates — they write to different columns for different purposes
- "Company name" is the registered legal name (often from Companies House)
- "Trading name" is an optional alternative name the business operates under (e.g., "Acme Limited" registered, but trades as "Acme Builders")
- The form currently provides no explanation of when to use trading name vs leaving it blank

**Options:**
(a) Add helper text below "Trading name (if different)" field explaining: "If your business operates under a different name than its registered company name, enter it here. This will appear on contracts instead of your registered company name."

(b) Change the label from "Trading name (if different)" to something clearer like "Operating name (if different from company name)" with brief helper text

(c) Reorder the sections to place trading name closer to company name for easier comparison

(d) Keep as-is — the "(if different)" qualifier is sufficient and most contractors will understand the distinction

**Recommendation:** Option (a). Adding brief explanatory text is the minimal change that addresses the UX concern without restructuring the form or changing frozen labels. It's reversible, doesn't affect data flow, and helps contractors understand when they should fill in this optional field.
