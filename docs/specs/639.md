# REFUND-2: Refund on a staged job

## Problem

REFUND-1 treats every job as a single settlement. STAGE-2 introduced multiple settlements per job — deposit and balance stages, each with its own Stripe payment intent. A trade that needs to refund a staged job cannot choose which stage to reverse, and refunding "the job" is ambiguous when two separate payments have landed.

The trade needs to refund the deposit alone, the balance alone, or both, without reopening stages that stay settled.

## Scope: what changes

**Migration**: Extend `payment_stages` with refund-tracking columns mirroring what `jobs` already has — `payment_provider_ref`, `settlement_state`, and `total_refunded_pennies`. Each stage tracks its own Stripe settlement and refund state independently.

**Library**: Add stage-aware refund functions to `refund-settlement.ts` — `getStageRefundEligibility` and `refundStage` — that operate on a single stage rather than the whole job. Update `payment-stages.ts` types to include refund fields and extend `deriveStageStatuses` to show refund state.

**Server actions**: Add `checkStageRefundEligibility` and `processStageRefund` to `refund-actions.ts`, mirroring the job-level actions.

**UI**: Extend `refund-button.tsx` to recognize staged jobs and present a stage picker. Once a stage is selected, the refund dialog behaves identically to REFUND-1 — same amount input, same quick-select buttons, same balance warning. The trade can refund one stage and leave immediately, or return to refund the other.

The job-level refund functions stay unchanged and continue to work for single-payment jobs. Nothing is deprecated.

## Explicitly out of scope

- Refunding multiple stages in a single operation. The trade refunds one stage at a time, and can do so in any order.
- Changing how refunds affect the job's `paid_at` or overall pipeline state. Refunding one or both stages leaves the job in whatever state the remaining settled stages imply.
- Any change to the balance warning wording or presentation. The same warning REFUND-1 established appears for each stage refund.

## Edge cases

**A job with no stages** continues to use the job-level refund path. The stage picker does not appear.

**A job with one stage settled and one not** allows refunding the settled stage without waiting for the other to settle. The unsettled stage does not become refundable until it settles.

**Partial refund within a stage** works identically to REFUND-1 — the trade enters an amount, sees "Everything" and "Half" quick-select buttons, and the stage lands in `partially_refunded` state if the amount is less than the stage's settlement.

**Refunding both stages in sequence** leaves each stage in its own state (`refunded` or `partially_refunded`) based on what was returned from it. The job's overall state derives from the stages, unchanged from STAGE-2.

**A stage with no `payment_provider_ref`** (manually marked paid) is ineligible for refund, exactly as a manually-marked job is in REFUND-1. The eligibility check returns the same "No settlement to reverse" reason.

**A stage paid via TrueLayer** (`tl_…` prefix) is refused rather than sent to Stripe, with the same "didn't go through Stripe" message REFUND-1 established.

## Risks

**Money movement** — every risk from REFUND-1 applies per-stage. The ceiling comes from Stripe; `reverse_transfer: true` debits the trade's account; `refund_application_fee: false` does not return motko's fee. The 2026-09-05 decision in `areas/motko.md` states this applies to REFUND-2 by precedent.

**Migration timing** — schema must precede code. Apply the migration to production before merging the PR, or the stage refund actions will fail on missing columns.

**RLS policies** — the `payment_stages` policies scope to the trade's own jobs. The refund actions pass the user-scoped Supabase client, so RLS is the second lock (the session check is the first).

## Derived criteria

- A staged job's refund control lets the trade choose which stage to refund
- Refunding the deposit stage does not affect the balance stage, and vice versa
- Each stage refund shows the same "This comes out of your account" warning REFUND-1 established
- Partial refunds within a stage work identically to job-level partial refunds — amount input, "Everything" and "Half" buttons, `partially_refunded` state when not fully returned
- A stage with no Stripe settlement (manual payment or TrueLayer) is ineligible and says why
- The refund ceiling for a stage comes from Stripe, queried live, never from our own columns
- `reverse_transfer: true` and `refund_application_fee: false` apply to every stage refund
- A job with no stages continues to use the job-level refund path unchanged
- Refunding all stages does not reopen the job or change its pipeline state beyond what the stage settlements imply
