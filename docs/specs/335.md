# Issue #335: FEE-4: Replace "free while in early access" with "your first three jobs are free", and write /pricing

## Problem

The marketing site motko.co.uk currently either doesn't exist or carries the withdrawn "free while in early access" promise. This open-ended claim has no defined end date, no defined population, and creates a mismatch with the product which already charges fees silently once a contractor exhausts their free-job allowance. The site cannot answer the question every visitor asks: what does motko cost?

## Scope: what changes

1. **Create or update the motko.co.uk homepage** to state "Your first three jobs are free" using the exact number "three". Remove all instances of "free while in early access", "free during beta", or any other open-ended free-access wording.

2. **Create /pricing page** at motko.co.uk/pricing publishing:
   - The three-band fee ladder:
     * £2 per job up to £1,000
     * £6 per job from £1,001 to £3,000  
     * £10 per job above £3,000
   - Fees are VAT-inclusive (VAT is inside the fee, never added on top)
   - Fees are taken when the customer pays (deducted from their payment)
   - Nothing is charged until the contractor has been paid
   - Referral rewards: 3 free jobs per referral who completes their first paid job
   - Referral tier: after 5 successful referrals, earn 5 free jobs per referral (up from 3)
   - Credits stack (accumulate)
   - Free job waiver rule: a free job covers the standard £2 fee; on higher-value jobs (£6 or £10 band), the difference above £2 is payable

3. **Remove the word "beta" from any context that implies a price.** Beta as a product-maturity signal is acceptable; beta as a pricing excuse is not.

4. **Replace or remove existing screenshots, OG images, or PDFs** that contain the old "free while in early access" wording. Do not leave stale assets in place.

The marketing site should be implemented as a standalone static site in a new `/site` directory, to be deployed separately to motko.co.uk.

## Explicitly out of scope: what does not change

- In-app fee copy, the free-jobs chip, and the fees statement (covered by FEE-3)
- Grant values and tier logic in the product code (covered by FEE-1)
- The waiver split implementation (covered by FEE-2)
- Any email or in-app announcement to existing users
- Off-rail fee collection mechanisms
- The fee band values themselves (already settled: £2/£6/£10 at £1k/£3k thresholds)
- Changes to motko.app (the product app) — only motko.co.uk changes

## Edge cases

1. **Norfolk cohort grandfathering**: The site says "three" free jobs because that's what new signups get. Norfolk contractors and other early users were granted 5 free jobs and keep that balance. No correction, no announcement, no claw-back needed. Existing users getting more than the site promises is not a problem.

2. **No notice period required**: "Free while in early access" was never scoped to a population or date, and every existing user retains every credit they hold. The new copy describes what the product already does.

3. **FEE-3 dependency**: If FEE-3 (in-app fee surfaces) has not shipped when this goes live, the site will state a price the app does not display. This is worse than today. This ticket should not deploy ahead of FEE-3 — sequence them together.

4. **FEE-1/FEE-2 not yet shipped**: If the fee ladder in FEE-1/FEE-2 has not shipped at the time /pricing goes live, publish the ladder anyway. It is the committed model, and publishing before charging is the trust-building order. The ladder stated here (£2/£6/£10) is the settled model that FEE-1 will implement.

5. **Mid-signup contractor**: A contractor signing up when the copy changes is unaffected. The grant is set at account creation server-side, not determined by site copy.

6. **Existing screenshots/assets**: Screenshots in `public/marketing/` carrying the old wording must be regenerated or removed. Do not serve stale marketing materials.

7. **Empty state**: If motko.co.uk doesn't exist yet, create it with the correct copy from the start. If it exists with the old copy, update it.

## Risks

**Customer-facing pricing**: This changes public pricing claims. The values are settled (Jacob, 24 Aug 2026) and match what the product already charges, but the copy is what trades will see and quote when asking "how much does motko cost?"

**Deployment sequence**: Publishing this ahead of FEE-3 makes the mismatch worse (site shows price, app doesn't). Coordinate deployment.

**Constants alignment**: The numbers must match the constants in FEE-1:
- FREE_JOB_ALLOWANCE for new users (should be 3, currently 5)
- FEE_STANDARD_PENNIES = 200 (£2)
- FEE_LARGE_PENNIES will become 600/1000 (£6/£10) in FEE-1
- FEE_BAND_THRESHOLD_PENNIES will have two thresholds (£1k, £3k) in FEE-1

**Legal/contractual**: This is customer-facing pricing copy. The values are decided, but stating them publicly makes them binding.
