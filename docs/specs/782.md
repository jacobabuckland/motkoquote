# VOICE-U1: A labour line priced from the contractor's own rates is not "unsourced"

## Problem

Lines arrive carrying populated day rates and `unit_price: 0`, `unpriced: true` at the same time. The unsourced branch at `src/lib/compile-draft.ts:1242` zeroes and flags every line that matches no stated price, regardless of whether the line already has contractor-sourced provenance. A labour line priced from the contractor's own stored rates is marked unpriced and zeroed, blocking the send with "Labour isn't priced: no day rate was found for this job" — when the day rate was found, is on the line, and is the contractor's own confirmed number.

## Scope: what changes

The unsourced branch at `compile-draft.ts:1242` gains a check before zeroing a line. **Two kinds of line survive it**, and both are named here because the narrower reading of this paragraph has already cost three cycles:

1. **A line with `provenance.source === "contractor"`** — its number came from the contractor's own confirmed data (their stored rates, a rate card, a known material price), so there is nothing unsourced about it.

2. **A labour line carrying real rates, whatever its provenance says** — that is, one not already marked `unpriced: true` by `resolvePerson`. For a labour line, provenance describes the DAY COUNT, which is the only half the model supplies; the RATE is the contractor's own in every case. So a labour line whose days are `system-generated` still has a defensible figure, and `compile-draft.ts:659-663` already says so in the tree: *"unlike a material with no price behind it, a labour line has a real rate and a defensible figure, so zeroing it would replace a number worth checking with no number at all."* That comment is the rule this item is restoring; the unsourced branch overrides it 580 lines later, unread.

A material line with no price behind it keeps being zeroed and flagged — that rule is correct and unchanged. A labour line with NO rate at all keeps being zeroed too: `resolvePerson` sets `unpriced: true` when `rateFound === false`, and (2) deliberately does not reach it.

## Explicitly out of scope: what does not change

- `resolvePerson` / `rateFound`: the missing-rate path is correct as written. A labour line that has no rate at all is genuinely unpriced.
- Materials with no price behind them: still zeroed, still flagged. A material with no contractor-confirmed price has no provenance source of "contractor".
- Whether `provenanceChecksEnabled` should test transcript presence rather than stated-price count: possibly; not this card.
- The wording of `UNRESOLVED_RATE_FLAG`: right for the case it was written for.

## Files

- `src/lib/compile-draft.ts` (modify): add provenance check before zeroing lines in the unsourced branch
- `tests/acceptance/782.test.ts` (new): acceptance tests

## Edge cases, including failure modes and empty states

- A labour line with `provenance.source === "system-generated"` and `unpriced: true` still blocks: the days are the model's and the rate is missing, so there is nothing defensible about the figure.
- A material line with no provenance is still zeroed: it has no confirmed price behind it.
- A line with `provenance.source === "transcript"` is already tied to a stated price, so it will match one and never reach this branch.
- An empty quote (no lines at all) is unchanged: it has its own guard (`ZERO_TOTAL_CONFIRM_REQUIRED`), not this one.

## Risks: anything touching money, migrations, or user data

**Money surface**: This moves a quote total from £0.00 to the contractor's own stored rate times the captured days. It is a restoration, not a reprice — the figure was always the contractor's number, and the £0 was never charged because the quote could not be sent. No rate, no markup, no fee changes.

## Derived criteria

1. A labour line priced from the contractor's own stored rates is never zeroed or marked unpriced because it matched no stated price. The day count or crew breakdown may have been captured, the rates were looked up, and the amount is defensible — there is nothing unsourced about it.

2. A labour line priced from a rate card is likewise never zeroed or marked unpriced because it matched no stated price. The rate card is the contractor's own confirmed pricing.

3. A material line priced from a known contractor-confirmed price is never zeroed or marked unpriced because it matched no stated price. `provenance.source === "contractor"` says the price came from the contractor's own data.

4. A material line with no price behind it (no provenance, or provenance but no confirmed price) keeps being zeroed and flagged. The rule is right and stays unchanged.

5. A labour line with no rate at all (`unpriced: true` set by `resolvePerson` when `rateFound === false`) still blocks the send. That is genuinely unpriced.

6. A quote whose only "unpriced" line was one carrying contractor-sourced provenance can now be sent, and no flag tells the contractor to add a day rate they already have.

7. The scenario from the Notion card (scenario-1's own contractorContext, labour draft, two runs differing only in stated-prices) is corrected: the same crew, same days, same draft produces £2,200 of labour whether or not two other stated prices were mentioned on the call.
