# FEE-8: Show the fee before payment as well as after

## Problem

The motko fee is stated in two places and absent from two. A contractor sees what a job costs them on the mark-as-paid sheet and on a paid job, but learns nothing when they send a quote or raise an invoice — the two moments they are committing to work at a price. On a £22,000 job that is £43.00 arriving unannounced once the customer has already paid.

## Scope: what changes

1. **A sent quote** (the `quote_sent` situation) states the motko fee the contractor will be charged when the job is paid, in the Next step card.

2. **An unpaid invoice** (the `invoice_unpaid` and `invoice_overdue` situations) states the same figure, same card, same wording.

3. Both derive the fee by calling `motkoFeePennies(netSubtotalPennies, freeJobsRemaining)` — the same function `markPaidFeeLine` calls — on the quote's NET subtotal (not the VAT-inclusive total).

4. **A free-job credit is stated where it applies**, in the same terms `markPaidFeeLine` uses: "no service fee" when the credit covers it all, or "covers £X of the £Y service fee, so £Z applies" when the waiver is partial (unreachable today under the unbounded ceiling, but the wording survives for when a ceiling returns).

5. The new line lives in `src/lib/fee-copy.ts` as a pure function beside `markPaidFeeLine` and `paidJobFeeLine`, asserted on its return value.

## Explicitly out of scope

- **Changing any amount.** This is display only. The fee charged at settlement is unchanged.

- **The customer-facing quote and invoice pages** (`/q/[id]` and `/i/[id]`). Out of scope; do not modify them.

- **Draft quotes** (not yet sent). No fee is stated. A draft has no committed amount and nothing is owed.

- `/pricing` and `/terms` — already updated by FEE-9.

- The waiver ceiling — FEE-11. If FEE-11 lands first, the free-credit wording follows it automatically because both read the same constant.

## Files

- `src/lib/fee-copy.ts` (modify) — a new `projectedFeeLine` function alongside `markPaidFeeLine` and `paidJobFeeLine`
- `src/lib/fee-copy.test.ts` (modify) — pure assertions on the new function's return value
- `src/app/jobs/[id]/page.tsx` (modify) — render the line in the `quote_sent`, `invoice_unpaid` and `invoice_overdue` situations
- `tests/acceptance/467.test.tsx` (new)

## Edge cases

- **A quote edited after send.** The fee follows the current line items (via `quoteNetSubtotal`), because settlement will. Do not freeze it at send.

- **A job with several unpaid invoices.** The fee is per payment, so it is stated once per situation rather than multiplied. The figure shown is what this one invoice will cost to settle.

- **A quote with no line items, or a zero/negative subtotal.** The fee floor applies — `motkoFeePennies` already guards this and returns £2.00. A projection that throws is worse than one showing the floor, which is what will be charged.

- **`freeJobsRemaining` is stale by the time payment lands.** The projection is an estimate. The wording says what will happen "when paid" or "when this is paid" rather than guaranteeing a figure, and the mark-as-paid sheet recomputes it at the moment the contractor acts.

## Risks

- **The line must match what settlement charges.** A mismatch between a stated fee and a charged one is the defect FEE-6 fixed. Both must call `motkoFeePennies` on the NET subtotal with the same `freeJobsRemaining` value, and the acceptance tests assert this by calling the function, not by restating an expected number.

- **VAT must not overstate the fee.** `quotes.total` and `sent_total` are VAT-inclusive. The fee ladder rates the NET base, so passing the total overstates the fee by a fifth for a VAT-registered trade. Derive the net subtotal via `computeQuoteTotals(lineItems, false).subtotal`, as the mark-as-paid sheet already does at line 250.

## Derived criteria

A plain-language checklist of what the ticket asks for:

1. A sent quote states the motko fee the contractor will pay when the job is settled.
2. An unpaid invoice states the same fee.
3. Both call `motkoFeePennies` on the NET subtotal, not the VAT-inclusive total.
4. Two quotes differing only by VAT registration produce the same stated fee (the NET subtotal is the same, so the fee is identical).
5. The stated fee before payment equals the fee `markPaidFeeLine` computes for the same job, same free-job balance.
6. A contractor with free credits sees what they will actually pay: "no service fee" when the credit covers it, or the payable remainder when it does not.
7. A quote with no line items does not throw — it states the £2.00 floor, which is what settlement charges.
8. The line is shown in the contractor's view of the job page, in the Next step card, for the `quote_sent`, `invoice_unpaid` and `invoice_overdue` situations.
