# PFIX-4: The first-run invention guard is switched off for essentially every account

**Approved:** 3 Sep 2026, Jacob  
**Module:** voice  
**Source:** [Notion PFIX-4](https://app.notion.com/p/PFIX-4-The-first-run-invention-guard-is-switched-off-for-essentially-every-account-3cf1e4f908b48152a369f7727f46c143)

## Problem

The 1 Sep decision says a first-run quote renders ungrounded prices as "to be confirmed", never as a figure, gated on whether the contractor has pricing history. That gate is defeated because a contractor who merely completed the business-setup interview has one knowledge chunk, retrieval returns it with no similarity threshold, and the gate reads "has pricing history" on their very first quote. On production, rate cards and confirmed material prices are empty for every contractor, so the guard is off everywhere. Every contractor-supplied material line is priced from a model-invented number with contractor markup applied. Reproduction: a model estimate of £180 becomes **£216.00** printed as a real price on a customer document, under "confirm against supplier price". The drafted breakdown—including invented estimates—is embedded into the knowledge layer at draft time, before any human sees it, so those figures return as "similar past jobs" in the next quote's prompt. The invention feeds itself.

## Scope: what changes

1. **Guest path declares no pricing history.** The guest quote path must set `has_pricing_history: false` explicitly, rather than leaving it unset and inheriting the permissive default. A guest has no pricing history by construction.

2. **A knowledge chunk with no price does not satisfy the pricing-history test.** `hasPricingHistory` (src/lib/pricing-history.ts:32-34) returns true today if any of confirmed material prices, rate cards, or similarPastJobs is non-empty. Retrieval has no similarity threshold, so a single business-setup chunk satisfies it. Change: the test is satisfied only by evidence carrying an actual figure for actual work—a rate card with a day rate, a confirmed material price with a price, or a past job whose content includes line items with prices. A price-free knowledge chunk does not count.

3. **Quotes embed into the knowledge layer only on approval or send, not at draft time.** Stop calling `syncQuoteKnowledge` (src/lib/knowledge.ts) from the draft path. Learn from what the contractor accepted or sent, not from what the model drafted. This stops the problem regrowing.

Both (a) the guest path setting the field, and (b) strengthening the predicate, must land together. Fixing (a) alone leaves real accounts printing invented figures; fixing (b) alone leaves the public funnel doing it.

## Explicitly out of scope

- Changing the retrieval similarity mechanism itself, or adding a similarity threshold to vector search.
- The fee ladder, and anything about what motko charges.
- How stated prices are parsed or attributed (PFIX-1, PFIX-2).
- Purging existing knowledge chunks already carrying invented figures. They are in production now—flag this as a follow-on if needed.

## Files

- `src/lib/pricing-history.ts` (modify) — strengthen `hasPricingHistory` predicate to require actual price evidence
- `src/lib/guest/quote.ts` (modify) — declare `has_pricing_history: false` in guest path
- `src/lib/knowledge.ts` (modify) — remove draft-time embedding call; retain approval/send-time call
- Wherever `compileDraftToLineItems` or the draft flow calls `syncQuoteKnowledge` today (modify) — if the call is in the draft path, remove it; if it's in the approval/send path, keep it
- `tests/acceptance/529.test.ts` (new)

## Edge cases

- **Contractor with exactly one past quote carrying invented figures** (drafted under old behavior): that quote should not count as pricing evidence. Prefer a false negative—a flagged blank—over inheriting a fabricated price.
- **Quote approved then edited down**: learn from the final state the contractor stood behind, not intermediate drafts.
- **Quote never sent**: not approval. Must not write to the knowledge layer.
- **Guest who becomes authenticated mid-flow**: still has no pricing history. The field is about the contractor's account state, not the session's auth state.
- **Existing knowledge chunks with invented figures**: they exist on production now. Acknowledge this in the PR description; do not silently purge them (irreversible write, needs Jacob). This ticket stops new ones forming.

## Risks

- **Changes behavior for all contractors**: an established account with pricing history is unaffected, but a genuinely new contractor will see more blanks on their first quote. This is correct per the 1 Sep decision, but is user-visible.
- **Knowledge layer write timing shifts**: existing behavior may assume draft-time embedding. Audit callers of `syncQuoteKnowledge` to confirm the move to approval-time is safe.
- **Guest path behavior changes**: the public unauthenticated funnel today prints invented figures. After this lands, it prints "to be confirmed". Review rendered output on `/guest/quote` before releasing to the tester cohort.

## Derived criteria

What I understood this ticket to require:

1. A contractor with no rate card, no confirmed material price, and no past quoted job does not receive material lines carrying figures the model invented. Such lines render as unpriced and flagged, per the 1 Sep decision.

2. Completing the business-setup interview does not, by itself, cause that contractor's next quote to carry invented material prices. A price-free knowledge chunk (one with no rate, no line item price, no confirmed figure) is not pricing evidence.

3. A contractor who genuinely has a rate card with a day rate, a confirmed material price with a price, or a past quote with priced line items still gets estimates as they do today. Established accounts are unaffected.

4. The knowledge layer gains no entry from a quote that has not been approved by the contractor. Drafts do not write; approval or send does.

5. An invented estimate cannot appear as "similar past jobs" context in a later draft, because unapproved drafts no longer embed.

6. The reproduction is closed: a model estimate of £180, with contractor markup applied, does not print as **£216.00** on a customer document. A no-history contractor gets an unpriced, flagged line instead, and the invented figure appears nowhere.

7. The absence of pricing history remains a first-class input to pricing—a deliberate state carrying information—not an absence to be filled or assumed away. This restores the 1 Sep decision's precedent.
