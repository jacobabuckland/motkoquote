# PRICE-2: Draft from locked line items, generating descriptions only

## Problem

The drafting model discards tradesperson pricing and rebuilds jobs at invented rates. On the reviewed run: £520 consumer unit labour dictated, rendered as £600 bundled into 2 days; £180 board rendered as £270 with "Board cost £180 as agreed" underneath; £85 each fitted × 2 = £170 rendered as £15.00; £160 outdoor socket rendered as £22.50; £90 bonding produced no line, showing £37.50 earthing materials instead. Over-charges and under-charges netted to £57 gap—close by coincidence, not accuracy.

PRICE-1 created the stated_prices record. This item makes the drafting model consume it as immutable: spoken prices become locked line items at exact figures, model writes descriptions only.

## Scope: what changes

The drafting model receives stated_prices as locked line items it may not alter, and generates descriptions only. Three decisions implemented:

- **D1**: Spoken price is locked—appears as line item at exactly that figure. Model writes description; may not alter, decompose, re-derive or supersede the amount.
- **D4**: Model may not invent monetary amounts. Line with no source in the record gets no plausible number (PRICE-3 decides what it gets instead; until then, suppressed).
- **D6**: Trade cost not customer-facing. Where fitted price is stated, quote shows that price—not decomposition exposing material margin.

Qualifier semantics binding (D3): `each` × quantity, `fitted` means no separate labour/materials line for that item, `already_paid` and `excluded` produce no chargeable line at all.

Amount applied deterministically in code, not asked for in prompt. Prompt instruction not to change a number is not a lock—model returns descriptions, code attaches amounts.

### Implementation approach

1. **Drafting prompt (src/lib/claude.ts)**: When stated_prices exist for this job, system prompt changes to instruct model that certain items have locked prices. Model emits descriptions only for those items, no amounts.

2. **Compilation (src/lib/compile-draft.ts)**: After model returns, `compileDraftToLineItems` applies locked amounts from stated_prices record:
   - Match draft line descriptions to stated price items
   - Override computed amounts with stated amounts
   - Apply qualifiers: `each` multiplies by quantity, `fitted` prevents splits, `already_paid`/`excluded` suppress chargeable lines
   - Unmatched stated prices flag as missing (PRICE-3 territory)
   - Draft lines with no stated price proceed as today

3. **Reader (src/lib/voice/stated-prices.ts)**: Export helper to get active (non-superseded, non-excluded, non-already_paid) stated prices for matching.

## Explicitly out of scope: what does not change

- **Provenance references and £0/TBC treatment**: PRICE-3
- **Reconciliation gate and send-time checks**: PRICE-4. This item must not add send-time check.
- **"As agreed" language, invented specification, double-charge detection**: PRICE-5
- **Extraction**: PRICE-1's record consumed as-is. Missing fixture amount is PRICE-1 defect, reported not patched here.
- **applyAgreedDayRate and applyFixedPrice** in `src/lib/agreed-costs.ts`: Already deterministically override pricing engine from `agreed_costs` and `sow.pricing`, keep working exactly as they do. Where both mechanisms apply to same line, interaction defined in edge cases.
- **VAT, markup, totals arithmetic** in `quote-math.ts`: Locked amounts are net unit prices entering same arithmetic as before.

## Edge cases

### Stated price vs stated fixed total disagreement
Do not silently reconcile. `reconcileStatedPrice` in `src/lib/stated-price-guard.ts` already raises flag naming both figures when `sow.pricing.fixed_amount` disagrees with priced lines. That flag must keep firing. Contractor is only one who knows which is right.

### Stated price vs agreed day rate covering same labour
Agreed day rate is a rate; stated price for named item is price for that item. Stated price wins on line it names, `applyAgreedDayRate` continues to govern labour lines it doesn't name.

### `fitted` on item model also wants to split
Split refused. Fitted price is one line.

### `each` with quantity model got wrong
Quantity comes from transcript, not model. If no quantity stated, line renders at unit price with quantity 1 rather than guessed count—PRICE-3 will flag it.

### Zero stated prices in transcript
Drafting model behaves exactly as today. This item must not change quoting for job where contractor named no figures—common case, not broken.

### Amount marked `already_paid` or `excluded`
No chargeable line. On reviewed transcript EICR at £120 correctly excluded already; behaviour must survive.

### Matching draft lines to stated prices
Fuzzy match on item description (normalize, compare words). Draft line "Consumer unit replacement" matches stated price item "consumer unit". Match stated price to first draft line that plausibly names it—one stated price applies to at most one draft line.

### Stated price with no matching draft line
Model didn't draft anything for that item. PRICE-3 decides flagging; this item documents it happened (stated price unmatched).

### Draft line with no matching stated price
No locked amount—line proceeds through compilation as today (model's estimate, rate card lookup, etc).

## Risks

- **Money actually changes**: After this, customer-facing quotes carry different figures than today. That is entire point—current figures are wrong—but fixture assertions below are real acceptance criteria, not formality.
- **Quotes can go DOWN**: Reviewed run over-charged some lines, under-charged others. Contractors see quotes change both directions on same job. Correct behaviour, should not be softened.
- **Golden quote PDF will move** for any fixture carrying stated prices. Re-baseline deliberately in own commit, per note at top of `tests/regression/quote-pdf-golden.test.ts`.

## Derived criteria

The implementation must satisfy:

1. Fixture transcript produces line items at £520, £180, £170 (2 × £85), £160, £90
2. No line item carries monetary value absent from stated_prices record
3. £170 line is single line at 2 × £85, not two lines and not £170 with quantity lost
4. No separate labour or materials line generated for item whose stated price carries `fitted`
5. No line generated for amount marked `already_paid` or `excluded`
6. Given transcript with no stated amounts, drafted line items identical to before this change
7. Stated-price mismatch flag from `reconcileStatedPrice` still fires when `sow.pricing.fixed_amount` disagrees with priced lines
8. Amounts attached in code: drafting response returning different figure for locked line does not change rendered amount
9. When stated price exists for an item, that exact amount appears on quote—not model's invented figure, not decomposed into labour+materials
10. `fitted` qualifier prevents model from generating separate labour and materials lines for same item
