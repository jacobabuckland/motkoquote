# JOB-1: A job can only be archived at quote stage, so a cancelled or disputed one hangs in the pipeline forever

## Problem

Today archiving only reaches two points in the pipeline: a quote can be archived inline on the dashboard (via `status = "archived"` on quotes), and a resolved contract can be archived by swiping its dashboard row (via `archived_at` on contracts). A job whose contract is signed or whose invoice is out and disputed has no exit — it sits in the pipeline being counted, sorted by urgency, and chased. The only options are to leave it there or mark as paid something that was never paid. Real work ends in ways the pipeline has no state for: the customer cancels, the job is abandoned, the invoice is disputed and written off. The chase cron makes this worse than untidy: an unpaid invoice keeps generating reminder waves to the customer on a 3/7/14/21-day schedule, and the contractor cannot stop the app chasing a person they have stopped chasing.

## Scope: what changes

- Add `archived_at` column to jobs table, following the same shape as contracts (nullable timestamptz with a partial index on `archived_at is null`)
- Archive and restore server actions at `/app/jobs/[id]/job-archive-actions.ts`, following the contracts pattern (idempotent, write `archived_at` only, owner-scoped via RLS)
- Archive button component at `/app/jobs/[id]/archive-job-button.tsx` on the job page, reachable at every stage
- Archived jobs list page at `/app/jobs/archived/page.tsx` showing all archived jobs, restorable
- Filter archived jobs out of the working pipeline: update `/app/jobs/page.tsx` to exclude `archived_at is not null` from the active/paid/all buckets and show them only in the archived bucket
- Stop chasing archived jobs: update `/app/api/cron/chase/route.ts` to filter out invoices whose parent job has `archived_at is not null`
- Navigation convention: archiving from the job page is job-ending, so it navigates to the dashboard with a toast confirming the action (matching the quote archive pattern)

## Explicitly out of scope

- Cancelling, voiding or superseding a contract as a legal act. Archiving files away; it does not notify the customer or undo an agreement
- Refunds, credit notes, or any change to an amount charged
- Changing the existing quote archive (status field) or contract archive (archived_at column) controls — they keep working; this adds the job-level action
- Bulk archiving
- Changing what the customer sees or can do: a signed contract stays viewable at its link, an unpaid invoice stays payable at its payment link

## Edge cases

- **Archiving a paid job**: allowed — it is filing rather than abandonment, with no change to the payment record
- **Archiving while customer is mid-signature or mid-payment**: the customer's action must still complete and be recorded. Archiving hides from the contractor's list but does not close the door
- **Archive then restore then archive**: idempotent, no duplicate rows, no reminder waves resurrected. The archive action guards `archived_at is null` and restore guards `is not null`, so repeats match no row
- **Archiving a job with no quote yet**: allowed — an abandoned intake clutters the pipeline
- **Restoring an archived job**: returns it to exactly the stage it was at. Chase waves already sent still count against the existing cap — restoring does not buy a fresh set of attempts
- **Job archived while invoice is overdue**: chase cron skips it immediately on next run. No further waves are sent
- **Empty archived list**: shows an empty state explaining what archived jobs are and how to archive one

## Risks

- **Migration**: schema change must be applied to production before the code that reads it merges (per CLAUDE.md migrations protocol)
- **Chase behaviour**: archiving must immediately stop automated chasing. The cron must filter by `jobs.archived_at is not null` as it already filters by `quotes.status in ('archived', 'declined')`
- **RLS**: the existing jobs table RLS policy is row-level and column-agnostic (owner-scoped via contractor), so a new column is covered on read and write the moment it exists. No new policy needed (stated, not assumed — same rationale as the contracts migration)

## Derived criteria

What I understood the spec to be asking for:

1. A job can be archived from its job page at any stage (quote drafted/sent, contract sent/signed, invoice sent/overdue)
2. Archived jobs leave the working pipeline: they do not appear in the active jobs list, are not counted in summary totals, do not appear in urgency sorting
3. Archived jobs are restorable via a dedicated `/jobs/archived` page, and restoring returns the job to exactly the stage it was at
4. Archiving stops automated customer chasing for that job — an archived job with an unpaid overdue invoice produces no further reminder wave
5. What the customer can do is unchanged: signed contracts remain viewable, unpaid invoices remain payable, archiving revokes nothing
6. Archiving navigates deliberately: it is a job-ending action, so the row leaves the pipeline and a toast confirms it (following the quote archive pattern already recorded in CLAUDE.md)
7. The jobs table gains an `archived_at` timestamptz column with a partial index on `archived_at is null`, following the contracts migration pattern exactly
8. Archive and restore actions are idempotent: archive guards on `archived_at is null`, restore guards on `is not null`, so repeats match no row
