# Deposits and staged payments: ask, agree, then trigger on signature and on stage completion

Written by hand on 14 Sep after five PM derivations died on five distinct
causes. Each was derived against a *description* of the codebase rather than the
codebase, so each named something that is not there. This spec is written from
the tree.

## Problem

A deposit exists today only as `contracts.deposit_pct`, a number entered on the
contract **after** the customer has already accepted. Seven production contracts
carry it and two are at 1% on £7-8k jobs — £72 and £81, figures typed to clear
a required field rather than to ask for a deposit. The quote the customer
actually reads, at `/q/[id]`, never mentions a deposit at all, so every one of
those seven was agreed after acceptance or not at all.

The money that goes missing is the money agreed verbally and taken in cash,
because the app offered nowhere to put it.

## What already exists — do not rebuild any of it

- **`payment_stages` rows are created and linked.** `src/app/dashboard/actions.ts`
  creates them on demand for a job over the Pay by Bank ceiling and links the
  invoice to the first uninvoiced stage.
- **`createInvoiceRecord` already writes the link.** `src/lib/invoicing.ts` —
  `paymentStageId` in, `payment_stages.invoice_id` updated.
- **`createInvoiceRecord` is at `src/lib/invoicing.ts`**, not `src/lib/invoice.ts`,
  and already takes `(supabase, input)`.
- **`markWorkComplete` already exists**, in `src/app/jobs/actions.ts`, taking
  `{ jobId, complete }`. It is job-level and stays as it is.
- **Trigger 1 already exists.** `src/app/c/[id]/actions.ts` raises a deposit
  invoice on signature behind the existing `.eq("status", "sent")` race guard.
  It reads the wrong *source*; the mechanism is sound.
- **`invoices.payment_stage_id` does not exist and must not be added.** No
  migration creates it, nothing reads it, and the link is held on
  `payment_stages.invoice_id` in one direction.

## What this item adds

### The ask

`src/lib/quote-deposit.ts` parses what the trade types — a percentage ("25%")
or an amount ("£500", "1,250.00") — against the live quote total, and returns
pennies. Errors are **returned, never thrown**: this runs against a field a
person is typing into, and a half-typed "2" on the way to "25%" must not throw.

The quote editor asks under the total the deposit is a proportion of.
`/q/[id]` states it above the accept control, because accepting is what makes
it due.

### The first trigger reads the right source

`signContract` prefers `quotes.deposit_pennies` and falls back to
`contracts.deposit_pct`, so the seven existing rows keep working unchanged.

A **recorded zero raises nothing**. The trade was asked and said no deposit, so
a stray percentage typed on the contract must not override that. Null falls
through; zero does not.

### The second trigger

`markStageComplete(jobId, stageNumber)` claims the stage with a conditional
UPDATE — `.is("work_completed_at", null)` **is** the exactly-once guard — then
raises that stage's invoice through `createInvoiceRecord` with its
`paymentStageId`.

Stage invoices use `invoice_type` of `deposit` or `final`, **never a new
`"stage"` value**. The column is free text with no CHECK so `"stage"` would
store, but `deriveSituation` and `deriveStages` both branch on
`invoice_type === "deposit"` versus not — a `"stage"` value would count stage 1
of 2 as a *closing* invoice and tick Invoiced and Paid on a half-paid job, which
is the defect #739 fixed.

## Explicitly out of scope

- **`src/lib/payment-stages.ts`'s three stub functions.** `getPaymentStages`,
  `linkInvoiceToStage` and `settlePaymentStage` take no Supabase client, touch
  no database and return hardcoded objects built from their own arguments — one
  contains a literal £7,000. Nothing in `src/` calls them; their only callers
  are two frozen assertions in `tests/acceptance/623.test.ts` that check each
  returns the value it was handed. They are unsatisfiable as written and
  retiring them needs its own card. This item routes around them and leaves
  them untouched.
- Legal terms for deposits (forfeiture, refunds, cancellation rights).
- Editing a deposit after the quote is sent.
- Migrating the seven `contracts.deposit_pct` rows onto their quotes. Copying
  them would assert they were agreed at quote time, which is the one thing we
  know is untrue of all seven.
- Re-asking the deposit in the voice flow.

## Edge cases

- **Empty deposit field** — null, no deposit. Distinct from a recorded 0, which
  is "agreed at nothing" and suppresses the signature trigger.
- **Deposit over the quote total, or over the Pay by Bank ceiling** — refused at
  parse time with a message naming the actual constraint. A single payment over
  the ceiling cannot be taken on the rail at all, so the customer would be sent
  a demand they have no way to pay.
- **A save from a surface with no deposit control** — `depositPennies` is
  `undefined` and the stored value is left exactly as it is. It must never blank
  a deposit that was agreed.
- **Concurrent `markStageComplete`** — the conditional UPDATE matches one row;
  the loser returns success without raising a second invoice.
- **A stage already invoiced by the dashboard path** — completion is recorded,
  nothing further is raised.

## Risks

- **Money movement.** Both triggers raise invoices. Exactly-once matters, and
  both rely on a conditional UPDATE rather than a read-then-write.
- **Units.** `deposit_pennies` is pennies; `invoices.amount` is pounds. The
  conversion happens once, at `depositAtSignature`, and is asserted.
