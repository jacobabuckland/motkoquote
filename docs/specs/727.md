# Re-issuing a quote after the customer has agreed

## Problem

When a contractor edits a quote that the customer has already accepted, the customer's acceptance remains on record even though the quote has changed. This creates a mismatch: the customer believes they've agreed to specific terms, but those terms have been altered without their knowledge. The contractor has no way to void the acceptance and notify the customer that their agreement no longer stands.

## Scope

### Guard extension

`isEditableQuoteStatus` in `src/lib/quote-send-guards.ts` currently takes only a quote status and returns whether the quote can be edited. A quote is editable while `status IN ('draft', 'sent')`. Once accepted, the figures freeze—the contract's money panel reads `quotes.total` live, so rewriting after acceptance makes signed and proposed figures disagree.

Jacob's decision (13 Sep) adds a temporal boundary: a quote is editable through acceptance **up to the point a contract exists**. Once a contract is sent—signed or unsigned—the quote becomes read-only, because downstream state (deposit invoices, payment stages) now depends on it.

The rule is no longer expressible as a status list. An accepted quote with no contract is editable; the same quote with a contract sent is not. The guard gains a contract-presence parameter, and every caller supplies it.

### Three write paths

Every action that writes `quotes.line_items_json` or `quotes.total` must check editability:
- `updateQuoteLineItems` (src/app/jobs/actions.ts)
- `setQuotePricingMode` (src/app/jobs/actions.ts)
- `redraftJob` (src/app/jobs/actions.ts)

Each currently filters `.in("status", [...EDITABLE_STATUSES])` before reading the quote. That filter now under-selects: it excludes accepted quotes, including those with no contract. Each read widens to include accepted quotes, then applies the extended guard that checks contract presence.

### Re-issue behavior

When an edit is saved on a quote that was accepted but has no contract:

1. **Clear the acceptance**: `quotes.accepted_at` is set to `NULL`. The quote returns to an unaccepted state and requires fresh customer agreement.

2. **Update sent_total**: `quotes.sent_total` records what the customer was last told. `sentQuoteDivergence` (src/lib/sent-quote-disclosure.ts) compares it to the live total to decide whether to show a divergence notice. Leave it at the pre-edit figure and that notice fires permanently on the re-issued quote, telling the customer the document disagrees with itself.

3. **Notify the customer**: The contractor triggers the re-issue; the customer is told by email and SMS. The notification uses the approved copy (see below), names both the old and new totals, and explicitly states that the earlier acceptance no longer stands.

### Approved customer-facing copy

Approved 13 Sep by Jacob. This is on the AGENTS.md escalation list, so this approval is the human decision that unblocks it. An implementer may not vary it.

**Email subject**: Your updated quote from {company} — please accept again

**Email body**:
```
Hi {name},

{company} has updated the quote you accepted. The amount is now £{new} — it was £{old}.

Because the quote has changed, your earlier acceptance no longer stands. Please review the updated quote and accept it if you're happy with it.

[ View and accept your quote ]

If you were expecting the earlier figure, contact {company} before accepting.
```

**SMS body**:
```
{company}: your quote has changed — £{new} (was £{old}). Your earlier acceptance no longer stands, so please review and accept the new one: {link}
```

**Scope-only variant** (both channels): When the edit changes line items or scope narrative but leaves the total unchanged, the second sentence becomes:

```
{company} has updated the quote you accepted. The amount is unchanged at £{total}, but the details have changed.
```

Everything after it is identical. The "your earlier acceptance no longer stands" sentence is unchanged in every variant—it is the only place the customer learns their agreement is voided, so it may not be softened.

Use `formatMessageAmount(total, vatRegistered)` for every figure in these messages, not `formatGBP`—the existing send path does, and it's what keeps the VAT presentation correct for a registered trade.

## Edge cases

**No contract check before implementation**: Existing code allows editing accepted quotes if the guard is satisfied. After this lands, the guard refuses edits once a contract exists, even if the quote is still in an editable status.

**Race condition**: A customer accepts a quote between the editability check and the UPDATE. The UPDATE predicates on the same conditions (no contract, editable status), so the race is detected and the edit is refused.

**Total unchanged but line items or narrative changed**: The scope-only notification variant is used. The customer still sees both old and new figures are the same, but the sentence makes clear that details have changed.

**Customer has no email or phone**: The notification attempt is made but delivers to no channel. The quote is still marked re-issued (accepted_at cleared, sent_total updated), and the job page banner shows the "delivered to 0 channels" state with copy-link fallback.

**Contract exists but is declined**: A declined contract is still a contract. Once sent, the quote is not editable. The customer's signature was sought; downstream state may exist.

## Explicitly out of scope

- **#370's divergence notice** for quotes sent but not yet accepted. That is approved separately and covered by its own item.
- **Activity timeline preservation**. The spec states that the timeline should show both "Quote accepted" and "Quote re-issued", but the current `buildTimeline` function derives from current database state (quotes.accepted_at, quotes.sent_at, etc.). Clearing accepted_at removes "Quote accepted" from the timeline. Preserving the history would require either:
  - A separate quote_events log table that records status transitions
  - Storing the previous accepted_at timestamp elsewhere
  - A different derivation approach
  
  This is acknowledged as requiring additional implementation work beyond the core edit guard and notification requirements.

- **The header comment in src/lib/sent-quote-copy.ts**. The issue mentions updating the "⚠️ CUSTOMER-FACING COPY — PROPOSED, NOT APPROVED" banner, but this is a human checklist item for the implementer, not an acceptance criterion. Tests should not assert on source file contents.

## Risks

- **Customer confusion**: The re-issue notification must clearly state that the earlier acceptance no longer stands. The approved copy does this explicitly and may not be softened.
- **Data integrity**: Clearing accepted_at is an irreversible write. The old acceptance timestamp is lost unless preserved elsewhere.
- **Notification delivery**: If both email and SMS fail, the quote is re-issued locally but the customer is not informed. The job page banner surfaces this state.

## Derived criteria

1. A quote with status "accepted" and no contract can be edited
2. A quote with status "accepted" and a contract (sent, signed, or declined) cannot be edited
3. When an accepted quote with no contract is edited:
   - The accepted_at timestamp is cleared
   - The sent_total is updated to match the new total
   - The customer receives an email notification with the approved copy
   - The customer receives an SMS notification with the approved copy
   - The notification includes both old and new figures
   - The notification includes the sentence "your earlier acceptance no longer stands"
4. When the edit changes scope but not total:
   - The scope-only notification variant is used
   - The amount sentence reads "The amount is unchanged at £{total}, but the details have changed"
5. The guard checks contract presence by querying whether a contract exists for the quote_id
6. All three write paths (updateQuoteLineItems, setQuotePricingMode, redraftJob) enforce the extended guard
