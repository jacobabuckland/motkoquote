# Deposits and staged payments: ask, agree, then trigger on signature and on stage completion

## Problem

Trades routinely agree a deposit verbally and take cash outside the system, and Motko loses the payment entirely. The deposit_amount field on SowState is set on 0 of 25 SoWs. The deposit_pct field on contracts is set on 7, but two of those are 1% on £7,000+ jobs (£72 and £81 — numbers typed to clear a field), and the quote page never mentions a deposit: it first appears on the contract after acceptance, so all 7 were agreed retrospectively.

A deposit that reaches the accepted document and triggers a payment request on signature is enforceable and collectable. One stored but never mentioned or triggered is neither.

## Scope: what changes

**Voice intake**: After the checklist is satisfied and before the wrap, ask about deposits/tranches based on job value:
  - Under £5,000: "Would you like to take a deposit on this quote? If so, how much, or what percentage?" Motko computes the figure from whichever they give.
  - £5,000 and over: "Have you agreed a payment schedule in tranches with the customer? If so, what are they?" Motko calculates the tranche amounts and makes them sum exactly to the total.

The question is asked naturally as part of the conversation, not as a separate form step. If the contractor deflects ("I'll sort that later"), the slot stays unanswered and can be filled via the quote editor backstop.

**Database schema**:
  - Add `deposit_amount_pennies int` to `quotes` table (nullable) — the agreed deposit amount in pence, derived from either a stated amount or a percentage of the quote total
  - Reuse `payment_stages` table for tranched payment schedules (>= £5k jobs). Each tranche is a stage with its own amount, invoice, and settlement. The existing `stage_number`, `amount_pennies`, `invoice_id`, and `settled_at` columns handle tranches identically to how they handle £10k Pay-by-Bank splits.

**SoW schema**: Add `deposit` object to `sowStateSchema` and `sowDeltaSchema`:
```typescript
deposit: z.object({
  amount: z.number().positive().nullable().default(null),     // stated amount in GBP
  percentage: z.number().positive().max(100).nullable().default(null),  // or percentage
  notes: nullishString,
}).nullable().default(null)

tranches: z.array(z.object({
  stage_number: z.number().int().positive(),
  amount: z.number().positive(),           // in GBP, converted to pennies on quote creation
  description: z.string(),                 // e.g. "On signature", "On completion", "Mid-job"
})).default([])
```

**Quote compilation**: `compileQuote` in `src/lib/quote-compile.ts`:
  - Reads `sow.deposit` and computes `deposit_amount_pennies` (from amount or percentage of total)
  - Reads `sow.tranches` and validates they sum to the quote total (± £1 tolerance for rounding)
  - Writes `deposit_amount_pennies` to the quote row
  - For tranches: creates `payment_stages` rows for the job via `createPaymentStages`

**Quote display**: The quote PDF and `/q/[id]` page show the deposit amount BEFORE acceptance:
  - "A deposit of £XXX is due on signature" (when deposit is set)
  - Payment schedule table showing each tranche and its trigger (when tranches are set)

**Contract signature trigger**: `signContract` in `src/app/c/[id]/actions.ts`:
  - Reads `quote.deposit_amount_pennies` (not just `contract.deposit_pct`)
  - If set, raises a deposit invoice for that amount via `createInvoiceRecord`
  - For tranched jobs: raises invoice for stage 1 (the on-signature tranche)

**Work completion trigger**: New `markWorkComplete` action in `src/app/jobs/[id]/actions.ts`:
  - Sets `jobs.work_completed_at` to now()
  - For tranched jobs: raises invoices for remaining unpaid stages
  - For non-tranched jobs with a deposit: raises final invoice for (total - deposit)
  - Idempotent: re-marking complete does not re-raise invoices

**Quote editor backstop**: The quote editor at `/jobs/[id]` gains:
  - Deposit amount field (visible for jobs under £5,000)
  - Tranche schedule builder (visible for jobs £5,000 and over)

Both allow the contractor to set/edit deposit or tranches if the voice intake didn't capture them.

## Explicitly out of scope

- Forfeiture, refund, and cancellation wording on deposits (legal review needed, deferred)
- Charging on a tranche before its trigger event (signature or completion)
- Editing tranches after the quote is accepted (the schedule is fixed at acceptance)
- Materials-cost deposit proposal engine (superseded — Motko asks, never proposes)
- Changing how deposit_pct on contracts works (it stays, coexisting with deposit_amount_pennies)

## Edge cases

**Contractor states both amount and percentage**: Prefer the amount (more specific). If they conflict (20% of £4,000 is £800 but they said £1,000), record both and let the quote editor show the conflict for them to resolve.

**Deposit exceeds quote total**: Refused at compilation with "Deposit cannot exceed the quote total."

**Tranches don't sum to total**: Refused at compilation with "Tranches must sum to the quote total." Tolerance of ± £1 for rounding.

**Tranche exceeds £10,000 Pay-by-Bank ceiling**: Each tranche is validated against `PAY_BY_BANK_LIMIT_PENNIES`. Refused if any single tranche exceeds it, with "Each tranche must be under £10,000 to be payable online."

**Job has both deposit_amount_pennies AND payment_stages**: Not possible — tranches (payment_stages) are only created for jobs >= £5k, and deposit_amount_pennies is only used for jobs < £5k. A job transitioning across the boundary during editing picks one or the other based on final total.

**Re-marking work complete**: Idempotent. The action checks which invoices already exist and only raises missing ones.

**No deposit or tranches agreed**: Valid. Quote compiles with deposit_amount_pennies = null and no payment_stages. The contractor can raise invoices manually as they always could.

**Deposit agreed but contractor never asks customer to sign**: The deposit is shown on the quote but no invoice fires. Same as deposit_pct today — the figure is enforceable once accepted, but nothing happens until signature.

**Voice intake times out before deposit question**: The slot stays unanswered (deposit = null, tranches = []). Quote editor backstop allows setting it later.

## Risks

**Money movement**: Deposit invoices fire automatically on signature. A miscalculated amount invoices the customer incorrectly. Mitigation: `deriveInvoiceAmount` validates deposit against quote total, and deposit amount is shown on quote before acceptance.

**Migration timing**: Schema must precede code. Apply migration to production before merging PR.

**Exactly-once semantics**: `signContract` is already idempotent (state-machine guard on status = 'sent'). `markWorkComplete` must be too — check existing invoices before raising new ones.

**Tranche validation**: A contractor could dictate tranches that don't sum to the total. Compilation must refuse these rather than silently adjusting.

**Voice intake lengthening**: Adding a deposit question after checklist extends call duration. Acceptable because it's one question and it captures value currently lost. Contractor can defer ("I'll sort that later") if in a rush.

## Derived criteria

1. Voice intake asks about deposits for jobs under £5,000 and tranches for jobs £5,000 and over, after the checklist and before wrapping
2. A contractor stating "twenty percent deposit" has that captured as deposit.percentage = 20
3. A contractor stating "five hundred quid up front" has that captured as deposit.amount = 500
4. Quote compilation computes deposit_amount_pennies from either amount or percentage
5. The quote PDF and /q/[id] page show the deposit amount before the customer accepts
6. Signing a contract with a deposit raises a deposit invoice for that amount automatically
7. For tranched jobs, signing raises an invoice for the first tranche (on signature)
8. Marking work complete raises invoices for any unpaid tranches
9. Marking work complete a second time does not re-raise invoices (idempotent)
10. Tranches that don't sum to the quote total are refused at compilation with a clear message
11. A tranche exceeding £10,000 is refused with "Each tranche must be under £10,000 to be payable online"
12. The quote editor allows setting/editing deposit for jobs under £5k and tranches for jobs £5k and over
13. A job with neither deposit nor tranches compiles successfully (both are optional)
14. deposit_amount_pennies is written to the quotes table and read by signContract
15. payment_stages rows are created for tranched jobs and linked to invoices as they're raised
