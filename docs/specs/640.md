# STAGE-3: Extend automatic chasing to unsettled stages

## Problem

The automatic chase system (`src/lib/chase-plan.ts`, `src/app/api/cron/chase/route.ts`) chases unpaid invoices on a 3/7/14/21-day wave with a hard contact cap. It does not chase an individual unsettled payment stage, so a deposit with a due date that passes without payment is silent until the contractor manually creates an invoice for it. Once an invoice exists for a stage, the invoice chase handles it — but before that, nothing fires.

## Scope: what changes

**Database**: Add `due_date` (nullable `date`) to `payment_stages` so a stage can be overdue before an invoice is created. Add `stage_id` (nullable UUID FK) to `chase_events` and make `invoice_id` nullable, with a check constraint that exactly one is set — a chase event is about either an invoice or a stage, never both, never neither.

**Chase planning**: The pure planner in `src/lib/chase-plan.ts` is already I/O-free and takes a due date and a list of events; it does not change. The cron route loads both invoices and stages, plans each independently, and dispatches. Stages without an `invoice_id` are chased directly; stages with one are already chased via the invoice and are excluded from the stage chase.

**Contact cap**: The cap counts waves sent to a customer across BOTH invoice chases and stage chases. If an invoice has had 2 waves and a stage (same job, same customer) has had 2 waves, that customer has received 4 of the allowed 4 total waves — no more are sent for any outstanding item on that job. The cap marker is recorded once per job (or once globally per customer if job-level tracking is infeasible), not once per invoice and once per stage.

**Termination**: A settled stage (`settled_at` not null) stops being chased, matching the rule for paid invoices. An archived or declined job's stages are never chased, matching the existing invoice exclusion.

## Explicitly out of scope

- Chasing stages that already have invoices (the invoice chase handles them)
- Changing the wave schedule or cap value (reuse the existing 3/7/14/21-day waves and 4-wave cap)
- Notifying the contractor to create an invoice for an un-invoiced stage (this is customer chasing only)
- Setting stage due dates automatically when stages are created (that is a STAGE-2 follow-up; this item assumes the due date is set somehow and chases based on it)

## Edge cases

- **Stage with an invoice**: Excluded from the stage chase. The invoice chase already covers it, so chasing the stage would double-contact the customer.
- **Stage with no due_date**: Not chased (same rule as invoices — `due_date` null means no terms stated, nothing to chase against).
- **Settled stage**: Never chased, regardless of due date. `settled_at` not null means paid.
- **Archived job**: Its stages are never chased, matching the existing invoice rule (line 113 in the current route).
- **Declined quote**: Its stages are never chased, matching the invoice exclusion on `UNCHASEABLE_QUOTE_STATUSES`.
- **Contact cap reached**: Once a customer has received the max waves across invoices and stages for a job, no further customer contact happens for any outstanding item on that job. The trade is notified once (not once per invoice and once per stage).
- **Stage and invoice both overdue for the same job**: Both are eligible for chasing, but the cap still applies across them — if the invoice has used 3 waves, the stage can only send 1 before the cap is hit.

## Risks

- **Migration timing**: This adds a column to `payment_stages` and modifies `chase_events`. Apply the migration to production before merging the code that writes `stage_id` chase events.
- **Double-chasing**: If the exclusion logic is wrong, a customer could be chased once for the stage and again for its invoice. Mitigated by explicitly excluding stages that have an `invoice_id`.
- **Cap accounting**: If invoice chases and stage chases count separately, a customer could receive 4 waves for an invoice and 4 for a stage (8 total), which violates the spec. The cap must be per-job or per-customer, not per-invoice or per-stage.

## Derived criteria

1. A payment stage with `settled_at` null and `due_date` < today and no `invoice_id` produces a customer reminder on the 3/7/14/21-day wave schedule, exactly as an unpaid invoice does
2. A payment stage that already has an `invoice_id` is NOT chased via the stage path — the invoice chase covers it
3. The contact cap applies across invoice chases and stage chases together: if a customer has received 4 waves total (across all invoices and stages for a job), no more customer contact happens
4. A settled stage (settled_at not null) is never chased, even if overdue
5. An archived job's stages are never chased, matching the invoice rule
6. A declined quote's stages are never chased, matching the invoice rule
7. The existing 3/7/14/21-day wave schedule and 4-wave hard cap are reused unchanged — no new schedule or separate cap for stages
8. `chase_events` can record a chase for a stage (via `stage_id`) as well as for an invoice (via `invoice_id`), and exactly one of the two is set on each row
