# RAIL-2: Mark-as-paid stays unaffected and unpenalised

**Issue:** #586  
**Module:** payments  
**From:** motko-pre-launch-spec.md §5 D9, order 10 of 34

## Problem

Making the rail the default must not turn off-rail settlement into a second-class path. Motko does not police how a trade gets paid; it stops doing the customer's work for them. A trade paid in cash records it and the job closes properly — with the same terminal state as a rail-paid one, and without creating a fee record for a Stripe cost that was never incurred.

## Scope: what changes

Off-rail settlements (cash, bank transfer, other) now write **no fee record**. There is no Stripe cost for motko to recover, so recording a fee as "accrued" or otherwise was always wrong — it created a bill for money motko never spent.

The change:
- `planPaidJobSettlement` gains an `isOffRail` parameter derived from the payment method.
- When `isOffRail` is true, the returned fee outcome is all zeros with status `"not_applicable"`.
- `settlePaidJob` determines `isOffRail` from `input.paymentMethod` and passes it through.
- The logic remains **pure** — no database reads, same unit-testable shape.

A manually-paid job reaches the same terminal state as a rail-paid one: `paid_at` is set, the invoice flips to paid, the job page shows it settled. The only difference is what does NOT get written: no fee amount, no accrual, nothing to collect.

## Explicitly out of scope

- **The free-job counter.** SUB-2 owns the rule: one counter, decremented by any completed job however it settled. Rail, cash, cheque or bank transfer — all decrement it. Assert **nothing** about whether an off-rail job decrements the allowance, in either direction. A frozen assertion here would collide with SUB-2 and make the two items mutually unsatisfiable.

- **The mark-as-paid surface.** No change to the UI. The sheet currently shows fee copy via `markPaidFeeLine` from `fee-copy.ts`; that line stays. What changes is only what gets recorded in the database after the trade submits.

- **On-rail settlements.** Stripe Pay by Bank and legacy TrueLayer payments are unaffected. They continue to record fees as they do today.

## Files

- **src/lib/paid-job-settlement.ts** (modify) — `PaidJobFacts` gains `isOffRail?: boolean`, and `planPaidJobSettlement` returns a zero fee outcome when it's true.

- **src/lib/settle-paid-job.ts** (modify) — derives `isOffRail` from `input.paymentMethod` and passes it to `planPaidJobSettlement`.

- **tests/acceptance/586.test.ts** (new) — regression test binding the no-fee-record invariant across payment methods.

## Edge cases

- **Free jobs still consume a credit.** An off-rail job with `freeJobsRemaining > 0` burns exactly one credit, same as an on-rail one. The counter is SUB-2's, but the consumption ledger entry is ours to write. The fee being zero does not skip the `job_consumed` event.

- **Referral activation is unchanged.** An off-rail first paid job activates a pending referral and credits the referrer, exactly as an on-rail one does. The fee being absent has no bearing on the referral path.

- **Staged off-rail jobs.** A trade can mark a deposit paid manually, then mark the final invoice paid manually too. Each settlement writes no fee record. The job closes after the second one.

- **Mixed settlement.** A deposit paid on-rail (accrues or collects a fee) and a final invoice marked paid manually (writes no fee) is valid. The fee columns on `jobs` hold the first settlement's values; the second writes zeros over them. This is correct: only the first payment settlement writes fee state, per the per-job guard at `settle-paid-job.ts:146`.

## Risks

None. This removes a wrong accrual — a fee for a Stripe cost that was never incurred. No money flow changes; off-rail jobs were not being collected from anyway.

## Derived criteria

1. A manually-paid job (cash, bank transfer, other) writes no fee record: `fee_amount_pennies`, `fee_net_pennies`, `fee_vat_pennies` all zero or null, `fee_status` is `"not_applicable"`.

2. An on-rail job (motko_bank, stripe_bank) continues to write a fee record as it does today, unchanged.

3. The mark-as-paid UI shows the same copy it shows today. No change to the sheet or button.

4. `planPaidJobSettlement` remains pure: no database I/O, unit-testable, deterministic.

5. The `isOffRail` flag is derived from payment method, not passed as user input or inferred from other state.

6. The ledger (credit_events) is unaffected: `job_consumed` and `referral_unlock` entries are written based on `freeJobsRemaining` and `isFirstPaidJob`, not on whether the payment was on-rail.

7. Assert NOTHING about the free-job counter decrement. SUB-2 owns that rule and states it correctly.
