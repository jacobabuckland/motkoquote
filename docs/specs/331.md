# FEE-2: Cap what one free job can waive at the base band

## Problem

Under the current fee settlement logic (`src/lib/paid-job-settlement.ts:79`), a free job credit waives the entire fee regardless of amount. With a flat £2/£4 fee this costs at most £4 per credit. With banded fees and unlimited credit stacking (FEE-1), one credit spent on a high-value job can waive the full band fee — potentially much more than the intended £2 base benefit. The referral programme's cost is unbounded in the one dimension that matters, and it is structurally the best-connected users who waive the most.

A free job should mean "the standard fee is on us", not "any fee at any value is on us".

## Scope: what changes

- **Database migration**: Add `jobs.fee_waived_amount_pennies int` to record how much of the fee was waived by a free-job credit. Nullable like the other fee columns (null until the job is first paid). Backfill existing waived jobs (where `fee_waived_reason = 'free_allowance'`) to record the amount that was waived.

- **Fee settlement logic** (`src/lib/paid-job-settlement.ts`): When `freeJobsRemaining > 0`:
  1. Compute the full banded fee for the job (via `motkoFeePennies`)
  2. Waive an amount equal to the base-band fee (read from the same fee configuration: `FEE_STANDARD_PENNIES` from `@/lib/motko-fee`)
  3. Charge the remainder: `payable = max(0, fullFee - baseBandFee)`
  4. Consume exactly one credit regardless of the payable remainder
  5. Record the split: full fee computed, amount waived, amount payable
  
- **Type definitions** (`JobFeeOutcome` in `src/lib/paid-job-settlement.ts`): Add `feeWaivedAmountPennies: number` to mirror the new database column. The existing `feeAmountPennies` becomes the payable amount (what's actually charged), and the sum `feeAmountPennies + feeWaivedAmountPennies` equals the full computed fee.

- **Stripe integration**: The payable remainder is collected through the existing Stripe application-fee path (via `application_fee_amount` on the destination charge). No second charge, no separate collection mechanism.

## Explicitly out of scope

- Grant values, tiering, activated-referral counter — FEE-1 handles those
- Any cap on the number of banked credits
- User-facing copy explaining the partial waiver — FEE-3 covers fee surfaces
- Retroactive recalculation of already-settled jobs (beyond backfilling the waived-amount column for auditability)
- The fee band values themselves
- Changes to how fully-charged jobs (no credit used) are handled — those continue unchanged

## Edge cases

1. **Full fee below base-band fee** (job ≤ £1,000 with credit):
   - Payable: £0
   - Waived: the full fee (£2)
   - Credits consumed: 1
   - Never record a negative payable or a waived amount larger than the fee actually due

2. **Full fee equals base-band fee** (job exactly £1,000 with credit):
   - Payable: £0
   - Waived: £2
   - Credits consumed: 1

3. **Full fee above base-band fee** (job > £1,000 with credit):
   - Payable: £2 (full £4 fee minus £2 base waiver)
   - Waived: £2 (capped at base-band fee)
   - Credits consumed: 1

4. **Payable remainder is non-zero but very small**: Charge it. Do not introduce a minimum-charge threshold.

5. **Multiple credits available on one job**: Still consume exactly one. Credits do not combine to waive more of a single job.

6. **No credits available** (already exhausted allowance): Charge the full banded fee as before. No change to this path.

7. **Legacy settled jobs**: Keep their existing rows untouched. This change applies to settlements occurring after deploy only. The migration backfills `fee_waived_amount_pennies` for historical waived jobs so they are auditable, but does not recalculate what was charged.

8. **Fee bands not yet repriced**: With the current single £2/£4 structure, the base band is £2, so a £4 job (> £1,000) waives £2 and charges £2. This is a real behaviour change and is intended — it is not gated on any reprice landing.

9. **Reversal or refund of a settlement with partial waiver**: Reverse the payable portion through the existing reversal path. Do not restore the consumed credit (consistent with FEE-1's monotonic treatment).

10. **The base-band value changes in fee configuration**: The waiver ceiling changes with it, with no code change required. Read `FEE_STANDARD_PENNIES` from `@/lib/motko-fee`, not a hardcoded constant.

## Risks

- **Money path**: This changes what contractors are charged on paid jobs when they have free credits. The ledger writes are real regardless of the `FEE_BILLING_ENABLED` flag (`src/lib/fee-billing-flag.ts:3-4`).
- **Database migration**: Must be applied to production BEFORE merging the code that reads/writes the new column, or the deploy will break (migrations do not run automatically on Vercel deploy — they are applied manually via `supabase db push`).
- **Stripe fee collection**: The payable remainder must flow through to the `application_fee_amount` calculation in the Stripe payment path. A missed integration means the fee is recorded but not collected.
- **Audit trail**: The new column must be written atomically with the existing fee columns to maintain referential integrity of the fee settlement record.

## Derived criteria

A correct implementation means:

1. A job in the base band (≤ £1,000) with a free credit available: fee charged is £0, one credit is consumed, `fee_waived_reason = 'free_allowance'`, and `fee_waived_amount_pennies` records the full fee that was waived.

2. A job above the base band (> £1,000) with a free credit available: fee charged equals the full band fee minus the base-band fee (e.g., £4 - £2 = £2), one credit is consumed, and the settlement records both the waived amount (£2) and the payable amount (£2).

3. A job above the base band with no credits available: the full band fee is charged (e.g., £4), no credit is consumed, no waiver is recorded, and `fee_waived_amount_pennies` is null or 0.

4. The payable remainder is collected through the same Stripe application-fee path as an ordinary fee — no second charge, no separate collection mechanism.

5. The amount recorded in `fee_waived_amount_pennies` never exceeds the base-band fee (`FEE_STANDARD_PENNIES`).

6. For any settled job: `fee_amount_pennies + fee_waived_amount_pennies` equals the full computed band fee for that job (within the constraints that waiving only happens with free credits, and the waived amount is capped at the base fee).

7. Changing the base-band value in `@/lib/motko-fee` (e.g., `FEE_STANDARD_PENNIES`) changes the waiver ceiling with no code change to the settlement logic.

8. Legacy jobs with `fee_waived_reason = 'free_allowance'` and `fee_amount_pennies = 0` are backfilled with a `fee_waived_amount_pennies` value for auditability.

9. The existing unit tests in `src/lib/paid-job-settlement.test.ts` are updated to cover the new partial-waiver scenarios.

10. The fee split columns (`fee_net_pennies`, `fee_vat_pennies`) are computed from the **payable** amount (`fee_amount_pennies`), not the waived amount — only what is actually charged carries a VAT split.
