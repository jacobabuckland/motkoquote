# NOTIF-2: The launch-critical notification events deliver on a real device

## Problem

A trade who does not know their quote was accepted, contract signed, or payment arrived has to keep checking the app — which is the behaviour the product exists to remove. Push notifications for these critical events exist but are incomplete: quote_viewed is tracked as analytics only, never notifies.

## Scope: what changes

Add `quote_viewed` to the notification events and wire it to fire when a customer opens a sent quote for the first time. The other three launch-critical events — quote accepted, contract signed, payment received — already send notifications and require no change.

Each notification carries customer name, job context and a deep link to `/jobs/{id}`, so the contractor can act without opening the app to work out what happened.

## Explicitly out of scope

- Permission timing and the pre-prompt flow (NOTIF-3)
- Stage-level payment notifications, which join with STAGE-3
- Testing notification delivery on a physical device from a cold start — this is a manual verification step, not an automated test (see below)

## Files

- `src/lib/schemas/notification.ts` (modify) — add "quote_viewed" to the events array and labels
- `src/app/q/[id]/page.tsx` (modify) — call `notifyContractorOfCustomerAction` when a sent quote is viewed for the first time, load `job.id` in the query

## Edge cases, including failure modes and empty states

- A quote in draft (never sent) does not trigger the notification, even when opened via its share link — mirrors the existing `viewed_at` guard
- Subsequent views of the same quote do not re-send the notification — it fires once, on first view
- A quote whose job has no customer record (deleted or malformed) renders "Your customer" as the name — consistent with quote_accepted/contract_signed
- The notification is best-effort: a failed send (no push subscriptions, delivery error) never throws and never blocks the page load

## Risks

None. This adds one event to an existing notification path and does not touch money, migrations or legal copy.

## Manual verification required

The acceptance tests verify the notification is sent with the correct payload. They cannot verify:

1. **Delivery on a physical iOS/Android device from a cold start** (app not running, not backgrounded) — requires installing the app on a device, killing it, and triggering a quote view to confirm the notification arrives
2. **Tap-through navigation** — requires tapping the delivered notification on a physical device to confirm it opens `/jobs/{jobId}`, not the dashboard

Both are human checklist items, not automated tests, per the guidance in AGENTS.md: *"Where the card names something outside the repository — an App Store listing, a marketing site, a physical device — no test can reach it."*

## Derived criteria

- `quote_viewed` appears in the `notificationEvents` array and has a label in `notificationEventLabels`
- When a sent quote (`status != 'draft'`) is viewed for the first time (`viewed_at` is null), `notifyContractorOfCustomerAction` is called with:
  - `event: "quote_viewed"`
  - `subject` and `heading` naming the customer
  - `jobId` pointing to the quote's job
  - A `nextStep` that makes sense for this event
- The notification is not sent on subsequent views of the same quote
- The notification is not sent for draft quotes
- Quote accepted, contract signed, and payment received already send notifications (regression check — no change to these paths)
