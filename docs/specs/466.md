# FEE-11: Redefine the free-job waiver ceiling — the ladder has no base band

## Problem

FEE-2 established that a free-job credit waives up to the base-band fee (£2 for jobs ≤£1,000) and charges the remainder. FEE-6 replaces banded fees with a marginal ladder, removing the structural concept of a "base band". Separately, FEE-7 introduces a processing fee that motko pays to Stripe in real money. Under FEE-2's partial waiver, a free job on a large-value job still charges part of the motko service fee. Under FEE-1's unlimited credit stacking, the cost of the referral program is unbounded — a contractor with 30 credits doing £10,000 jobs waives £750 in service fees.

The fix redefines a free job: it waives the **entire motko service fee** (regardless of job value) but **never the processing fee**. A cap on banked credits replaces the base-band ceiling as the bound on referral-program exposure.

## Scope: what changes

### Waiver logic (src/lib/paid-job-settlement.ts)

At the `freeJobsRemaining > 0` branch in `planPaidJobSettlement`:

1. Compute the full service fee via `motkoFeePennies` (as it does today, this will change once FEE-6's ladder lands).
2. Waive the **entire** service fee — `waivedAmount = fullFee`, not `Math.min(fullFee, FEE_STANDARD_PENNIES)`.
3. Payable service fee is always £0 when a credit is used.
4. Processing fee (introduced by FEE-7) is charged separately and is **never** waived. If FEE-7 has not yet landed, this item proceeds without processing-fee changes (the field will be zero); when it does land, the processing fee must be charged on every settlement including fully-waived ones.
5. Consume exactly one credit, regardless of the fee amount waived.
6. Persist the waived amount, payable amount, and processing fee (when FEE-7 introduces it) as distinct values.

Keep the split-fee machinery from FEE-2 rather than deleting it. The VAT split is computed from the payable service fee only (£0 when waived). The `fee_waived_amount_pennies` column records the full service fee that was waived.

### Banked-credit cap

1. **Add configuration constant** `MAX_BANKED_CREDITS` to `src/lib/motko-fee.ts` with a default value of **10** (confirmed with owner).
2. **Cap credit grants**: Modify `provisionNewContractor` in `src/lib/referral-signup.ts` and the referral-unlock path in `src/lib/settle-paid-job.ts` to enforce the cap. A grant that would take a contractor above the cap is **not applied** — the balance stays at the cap, the credit_event is not written, and the cache is not incremented.
3. **No clawback**: Existing balances already above the cap (if any) are not reduced. The cap applies only to new grants.
4. **Configurable**: Changing `MAX_BANKED_CREDITS` changes behavior with no further code change required.

The champion tier (5 credits per referral after 5 activated referrals, from FEE-1) is unchanged. Only the ceiling on what can sit banked changes.

### Processing fee coordination (FEE-7 dependency)

This item does not introduce processing fees — FEE-7 does. However, this item **must ensure** that when FEE-7 lands:
- The processing fee is charged on every settlement, including fully-waived ones.
- No settlement is ever net negative for motko (the processing fee is never waived).
- The guard in FEE-7 that prevents `application_fee_amount` from exceeding the payment itself continues to apply.

If FEE-7 has not yet merged when this item is implemented, the acceptance tests assert the waived-service-fee behavior and stub or omit the processing-fee checks. When FEE-7 merges, its tests must verify the processing fee is charged on waived jobs.

## Explicitly out of scope

- Grant values (3 on signup, 3 or 5 on referral unlock) — FEE-1 sets those.
- Retroactive recalculation of settled jobs.
- Off-rail jobs (PAY-7 / PAY-8), which have no processing fee.
- Marketing copy (FEE-9) about what "free" means.
- The marginal ladder itself (FEE-6) — this item works with whatever `motkoFeePennies` returns.
- Any UI changes to display waived vs processing fees — FEE-8 handles rendering.

## Edge cases

1. **Free job on a large-value job**: A £5,000 job with a free credit available results in service fee waived = £4 (the current full banded fee), payable service fee = £0, processing fee charged (when FEE-7 lands). One credit is consumed.

2. **Multiple credits available on one job**: Still consumes exactly one credit. The waiver amount is the full service fee for that one job.

3. **Contractor at the banked cap earns another credit**: The grant is not applied. The `credit_events` row is not written, and `free_jobs_remaining` stays at the cap. No error is thrown — the grant is silently declined.

4. **Contractor already above the cap** (e.g., had 15 credits before the cap was added): Keeps their 15 credits. They can spend them down. New grants are declined once they're back at or above the cap.

5. **Processing fee exceeds payment amount on a tiny job**: FEE-7's guard applies — `application_fee_amount` can never exceed the charge. This is FEE-7's concern, not this item's.

6. **Reversal of a waived settlement**: The consumed credit is not restored (FEE-1's monotonic treatment). The processing fee is not recovered (FEE-10's rule).

7. **Job paid in instalments with one credit**: The credit is consumed on the first settlement. Subsequent instalments on the same job are charged in full unless another credit is spent. (This is the current behavior; FEE-11 does not change it.)

8. **Waived amount + payable amount = full computed fee**: Always true. When a credit is used, waived = full fee, payable service fee = 0. The processing fee (when FEE-7 introduces it) is separate and is always charged.

9. **No credits available**: Full service fee plus processing fee (when FEE-7 introduces it) are charged. No waiver is recorded. This path is unchanged.

10. **Legacy settled rows**: Keep their existing `fee_waived_amount_pennies` values (from FEE-2's partial waiver). This change applies to settlements occurring after deploy only.

## Risks

- **Money path**: Changes what contractors are charged when they use a free credit. A credit now waives potentially more (the full fee on a large job) or less (nothing, if at the cap when the grant would fire).
- **Cap enforcement**: Must prevent grants, not claw back existing balances. A credit_event written despite the cap is a permanent ledger error (the append-only table cannot be edited).
- **Coordination with FEE-7**: Processing fees must be charged on fully-waived jobs. If FEE-7 merges after this item, its tests must verify that. If FEE-7 merges before this item, this item's tests must verify it.
- **Database integrity**: `fee_waived_amount_pennies` must remain non-null on every settlement after deploy, per FEE-2's requirement and this item's extension of it.

## Derived criteria

A correct implementation means:

1. A free credit waives the **entire** motko service fee, regardless of job value. A £5,000 job (currently £4 fee) with a credit results in £4 waived, £0 payable service fee, and the processing fee charged (when FEE-7 introduces it).

2. The processing fee (when FEE-7 introduces it) is charged on every settlement, including fully-waived ones. No settlement is ever net negative for motko.

3. When a credit is used: `fee_waived_amount_pennies` equals the full computed service fee, `fee_amount_pennies` (payable service fee) is £0, and one credit is consumed.

4. When no credit is used: full service fee plus processing fee (when FEE-7 introduces it) are charged, and no waiver is recorded.

5. A signup grant that would take a contractor above `MAX_BANKED_CREDITS` is not applied — no `credit_events` row is written, and `free_jobs_remaining` is not incremented.

6. A referral-unlock grant that would take the referrer above `MAX_BANKED_CREDITS` is not applied — no `credit_events` row is written, and `free_jobs_remaining` is not incremented. The referral still activates (status → 'activated'), but the reward is declined.

7. A contractor already above the cap (e.g., 15 credits) keeps their balance. They can spend it down. New grants are declined.

8. Changing `MAX_BANKED_CREDITS` in `src/lib/motko-fee.ts` changes the cap with no other code change.

9. `fee_waived_amount_pennies` is non-null on every settlement written after deploy. Assert against a real settled row, not just the planning function.

10. The VAT split (`fee_net_pennies`, `fee_vat_pennies`) is computed from the payable service fee (£0 when waived), not the waived amount. A fully-waived job records net = £0, VAT = £0.

11. FEE-2's base-band cap (`Math.min(fullFee, FEE_STANDARD_PENNIES)`) is removed. The waiver is the full service fee.

12. For any settled job: `fee_amount_pennies + fee_waived_amount_pennies` equals the full computed service fee (the processing fee, when FEE-7 introduces it, is separate and not part of this sum).
