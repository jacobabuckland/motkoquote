# SUB-5: Fee visibility on the trade's payment receipt

**Module:** payments  
**Risk:** `pricing` — displays money breakdowns on settled jobs  
**Source:** [Notion roadmap item SUB-5](https://app.notion.com/p/SUB-5-Fee-visibility-on-the-trade-s-payment-receipt-3d21e4f908b481b4ad80ce5d32e9010d)

## Problem

The trade's payment receipt has never existed. When a job settles, the contractor sees a single line stating the fee status but no breakdown of what the customer paid, what motko took, and what the trade receives.

This absence caused the 20 August incident where two live payments settled and the contractor saw nothing — no total, no fee, no net.

The current `paidFeeLine` shows only:
- "Paid in full. This was one of your free jobs — X left."
- "Paid in full. Motko service fee £X taken at payment."
- "Motko service fee £X — recorded, not charged."

None of these show the three-line breakdown the trade needs: customer paid → motko fee → you receive.

## Scope: what changes

A settled job (situation `paid`) shows a payment receipt section in the "Next step" card, immediately after the card title "Job complete — you've been paid", displaying:

1. **Customer paid:** the invoice amount (from `invoices.amount`)
2. **Motko fee:** the stored fee from `jobs.fee_amount_pennies`, or "Waived — X free jobs left" when `fee_waived_reason === 'free_allowance'`, or "£0.00 — no fee while in early access" while CLEAN-6 holds (`fee_amount_pennies === 0` AND `fee_status !== 'not_applicable'`)
3. **You receive:** customer paid minus fee

**Labelling requirement:** The fee line must use "Motko payment fee" or "Transaction fee", never just "Motko fee" or "Service fee", to distinguish the £9.90 capped payment fee from the £9.99 monthly subscription. A trade reading these two figures 9p apart must not confuse them as one charge or as a duplicate.

**Current paid-job surface:** src/app/jobs/[id]/page.tsx:510-524, the `case "paid":` branch. The `paidFeeLine` call at line 514-517 is replaced by the new receipt breakdown.

## Explicitly out of scope

- The fee amount itself (SUB-3 sets the rate; this item displays whatever is stored)
- The zero-fee interim (CLEAN-6 zeros the fee; this item says so plainly when rendering zero)
- The subscription state or amount (SUB-1's concern)
- Any change to how fees are computed or settled
- Jobs that are not yet paid (projected fees are already shown via `projectedFeeLine`)

## Edge cases

1. **Free job (waived):** `fee_waived_reason === 'free_allowance'` → show "Waived — X free jobs left" on the fee line, where X is `contractors.free_jobs_remaining` after this job burned its credit. Customer paid and You receive are identical.

2. **Zero fee (CLEAN-6 holding):** `fee_amount_pennies === 0` AND `fee_status` is NOT `'not_applicable'` → show "£0.00 — no fee while in early access" on the fee line. Customer paid and You receive are identical. This distinguishes a temporary zero from a waived zero.

3. **Fee collected:** `fee_status === 'collected'` and `fee_amount_pennies > 0` → show "£X.XX — taken at payment" on the fee line. Customer paid minus fee equals You receive, and the numbers must reconcile exactly.

4. **Fee accrued (not charged):** `fee_status === 'accrued'` and `fee_amount_pennies > 0` → show "£X.XX — recorded, not charged" on the fee line, matching the existing `paidFeeLine` wording for this state. Customer paid and You receive are identical because nothing was taken.

5. **Missing fee columns (legacy jobs):** `fee_amount_pennies` is null or `fee_status` is null → treat as zero fee, show "£0.00 — not recorded" on the fee line, and log a warning (these are pre-fee-column jobs and should be rare).

6. **Failed query:** `invoices` select fails (schema drift, phantom column) → the page already guards this via `throwIfQueryFailed` at line 147; nothing new needed here, but the tests must not silently pass on a null result.

## Risks

- **Money display:** this surface shows what the trade received and what motko took. The figures must reconcile exactly: `customerPaid - fee = youReceive` must hold to the penny, or the trade sees a discrepancy and loses trust.
- **Fee vs subscription confusion:** the £9.90 payment fee sits 9p from the £9.99 subscription. The labelling ("Motko payment fee" or "Transaction fee") is what prevents a trade reading them as one charge or as a duplicate. Weak labelling here is a support call.
- **Schema dependency:** reads `jobs.fee_amount_pennies`, `fee_status`, `fee_waived_reason` and `invoices.amount`, all of which exist on production today (verified via the phantom-column regression suite). No migration required.

## Derived criteria

1. A settled job shows a three-line payment receipt: customer paid, motko fee, you receive
2. The fee line distinguishes the payment fee from the monthly subscription via explicit labelling
3. A free job shows "Waived — X free jobs left" on the fee line and states the remaining allowance
4. While CLEAN-6 holds (zero fee, not waived), the fee line says "£0.00 — no fee while in early access"
5. A fee-collected job shows the amount taken and reconciles customer paid minus fee to you receive exactly
6. An accrued (not charged) fee shows "recorded, not charged" and reconciles customer paid to you receive (no deduction)
7. The receipt replaces the current single-line `paidFeeLine` in the paid-job next-step card
8. Legacy jobs with missing fee columns show "£0.00 — not recorded" rather than breaking
