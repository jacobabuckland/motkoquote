# One list of what's missing from the quote, not two

**Issue:** #735  
**Source:** [Notion roadmap item](https://app.notion.com/p/One-list-of-what-s-missing-from-the-quote-not-two-3da1e4f908b481eb9bd0e98f5d5bf58e)

## Problem

Two separate warning stacks on one scroll say overlapping things about the same quote:

1. **IncompleteCaptureCard** on the job page lists missing details including "the customer's name", "contact details", and "the site address"
2. **"Before you send" contractor flags** in the editor include "Missing customer details: name and a phone number or email. The quote can't be sent until these are filled in."

Both report missing customer details. Stating the same fact twice reads as noise.

The customer detail inputs (name, email, phone, site address) are all in the editor, so that's where the warning belongs — not on the job page where nothing can be fixed.

## Scope

**Change IncompleteCaptureCard to only show scope-related gaps**, never customer details:
- Keep showing: crew, duration, materials_supply, working_dates, deadline, agreed_costs
- Stop showing: customer_name, customer_contact, site_address

The editor's "Before you send" section already carries the customer-details flag and shows it where the fix happens. No change needed there.

**The criterion that matters:** A quote missing only the customer's phone number produces exactly one statement of that fact across the whole flow, on the screen where it can be fixed (the editor).

## Explicitly out of scope

- The wording or presentation of the contractor flags in the editor (unchanged)
- How `unasked_required` is populated upstream (unchanged — it may still contain customer detail slots)
- The `withCustomerDetailsFlag` logic (unchanged)
- What gets reported to `unasked_required` during voice intake (unchanged)

The filter happens at render time in IncompleteCaptureCard, not at the source.

## Files

- `src/app/jobs/[id]/incomplete-capture-card.tsx` (modify) — filter out customer detail slots before rendering
- `src/lib/schemas/sow.ts` (modify) — export a helper to identify customer detail slots
- `tests/acceptance/735.test.tsx` (new) — acceptance tests

## Edge cases

- **When only customer details are missing:** IncompleteCaptureCard renders nothing. The editor's "Before you send" section shows the gap.
- **When both scope slots AND customer details are missing:** IncompleteCaptureCard shows the scope slots only. Customer details appear in the editor.
- **When nothing is missing:** Neither card appears (existing behaviour, unchanged).
- **The cap-ended case:** When `capEnded` is true but no scope slots are missing, IncompleteCaptureCard may still show "The call reached its limit" — that's about call termination, not about missing details. Customer detail gaps still don't appear in that card.

## Risks

None. This is a presentation change only — no data model changes, no migrations, no new logic. The filter removes redundancy; nothing becomes unreported.

## Derived criteria

A contractor reviewing a quote sees:

1. **Scope gaps on the job page** (materials, agreed costs, crew, etc.) with a link to the editor to fill them in
2. **Customer detail gaps in the editor** (name, phone, email, address) in the "Before you send" section, where the inputs to fix them live
3. **Never the same gap in both places**
4. A quote missing only a phone number shows one warning, in the editor
5. A quote missing both materials_supply and customer_name shows materials on the job page and customer name in the editor
6. The existing test coverage for IncompleteCaptureCard (collapsed states, singular/plural, cap-ended logic) keeps passing with the filtered list
