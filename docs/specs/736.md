# The P&L says nothing until there's something to say

**Issue:** #736  
**Source:** [Notion roadmap item](https://app.notion.com/p/The-P-L-says-nothing-until-there-s-something-to-say-3da1e4f908b4819e9707de6fece9e521)

## Problem

On a job that has not been invoiced and has no costs recorded — which is every job for most of its life — the P&L card renders three rows showing "Not yet invoiced", a bold £0.00 for costs, and a "—" placeholder for gross profit. Two of the three values are placeholders, and the only figure set large and bold is a £0.00 that means "nothing entered yet", not "nothing spent". It reads as a result when it is an empty state, and it is the most visually prominent number on the lower half of the screen.

## Scope: what changes

The `JobPnL` component (`src/app/jobs/[id]/job-pnl.tsx`) gains an empty state. When there is nothing invoiced (`!hasInvoice`) AND no costs recorded (`costsNet === 0`), replace the three-row breakdown with a single message saying so and pointing at adding costs as the job runs.

Once either side has a real value (there's an invoice OR there are costs), the full three-row breakdown returns unchanged. A genuine £0.00 cost — a job where costs were deliberately recorded and happen to sum to zero — is a real result and must still show the full breakdown, not the empty state. The condition is `costsNet === 0`, not "no cost records exist".

The "Estimate only, not tax advice" footnote stays in both states.

## Explicitly out of scope: what does not change

- The arithmetic and VAT treatment
- The Costs section above the P&L card
- The P&L data query logic in `pnl-actions.ts`
- The component's props signature

This is the empty state only.

## Files

- `src/app/jobs/[id]/job-pnl.tsx` (modify) — add the empty state condition

## Edge cases, including failure modes and empty states

| State | hasInvoice | costsNet | Display |
|---|---|---|---|
| Job just created, nothing done yet | false | 0 | Empty state message |
| Costs recorded, no invoice yet | false | > 0 | Full three-row breakdown |
| Invoice sent, no costs recorded | true | 0 | Full three-row breakdown |
| Invoice sent, costs recorded | true | > 0 | Full three-row breakdown |
| Multiple costs that sum to exactly £0.00 | false or true | 0 | Full breakdown (genuine result) |

The `data === null` guard already handles the error state ("Unable to load P&L data") and is unchanged.

## Risks

None. This is a display-only change with no arithmetic, persistence, or money calculation involved. The condition gates what is shown; the values returned by `getJobPnL` are unchanged.

## Derived criteria

1. A job with no invoice and no costs recorded shows no bold currency figure in the P&L card — instead, a message explaining the empty state and pointing at adding costs
2. The same job, with one £40 cost added, immediately shows the full three-row breakdown including that £40
3. A job with an invoice but no costs shows the full breakdown (not the empty state)
4. The "Estimate only, not tax advice" footnote appears in both the empty state and the full breakdown
5. The component still handles `data === null` with "Unable to load P&L data"
