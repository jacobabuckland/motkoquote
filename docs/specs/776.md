# JOB-3: The signed contract is reachable from the job it belongs to

**Source:** Pass-7 review, 15 Sep — SERIOUS 5. Audited against main @ 44db45c.

## Problem

A job whose contract has been signed shows "Contract signed 14 Sept 2026" in its Activity timeline, but the job page carries no way to open that contract. The only route is Dashboard → Signed & declined contracts → Archived (16). A contractor asked for the signed contract mid-dispute — which is when it is asked for — will not find it where they look, which is on the job.

The job page already computes the contract's URL (line 357 of `src/app/jobs/[id]/page.tsx`). It is used for exactly one thing: a Copy contract link button, and only while the contract is sent and awaiting signature. The moment it is signed, the page stops offering any route to it at all — the state in which it matters most is the state with no link.

This is a surfacing item, not a plumbing one.

## Scope: what changes

1. The "Copy contract link" button (ShareLinkButton) appears wherever a contract exists — in ALL states from `contract_sent` onwards, not only while `status = "sent"`. States: `contract_sent`, `contract_declined`, `signed_need_invoice`, `work_complete`, `invoice_unpaid`, `invoice_overdue`, `paid`.

2. A "Download contract" link (InlineLink) appears wherever a contract exists, using the existing PDF route `/api/contracts/[id]/pdf`. This matches how quotes offer "Download quote" at all stages (line 1193-1200 of the job page).

3. Both controls appear in the existing "Actions" card, alongside the existing controls for each state. Where multiple actions exist in a state (e.g. `signed_need_invoice` has MarkCompleteButton and CreateInvoiceForm), the contract links appear AFTER the state's primary actions, so they do not compete for attention with the immediate next step.

## Explicitly out of scope

- What the contract page itself shows. Unchanged.
- Withdrawing or correcting a contract — that is CONTRACT-1, blocked on a ruling.
- The dashboard's archived-contracts list. Unchanged.
- Changing the contract URL or PDF route. Both exist and work; this item surfaces them on the job page.

## Files

- `src/app/jobs/[id]/page.tsx` (modify) — add contract link and download controls in all states where a contract exists
- `tests/acceptance/776.test.tsx` (new) — acceptance tests

## Edge cases

- **No contract exists**: the controls do not appear. This is already handled: `contractUrl` is null until a contract exists, and the rendering guards on that.
- **Contract sent but not yet signed**: both controls appear (no change from current behaviour for the link; download is new).
- **Contract declined**: both controls appear. A declined contract is still a document the contractor may need to reference.
- **Signed contract**: both controls appear. This is the primary fix — the signed state is when the contractor is most likely to need the contract again.

## Risks

None. This is a surfacing change to existing, working routes. The contract URL and PDF endpoint both exist and are authenticated; the job page already constructs the URL. This change makes it available in states where it was previously hidden.

## Derived criteria

1. A job with a sent contract (status = "sent") shows "Copy contract link" and "Download contract" in the Actions card.
2. A job with a signed contract (status = "signed") shows "Copy contract link" and "Download contract" in the Actions card.
3. A job with a declined contract (status = "declined") shows "Copy contract link" and "Download contract" in the Actions card.
4. A job with no contract does not show contract links.
5. The existing "Copy contract link" button in the `contract_sent` state continues to work as it does today.
6. The contract links appear AFTER the state's primary actions (e.g., in `signed_need_invoice`, they appear after MarkCompleteButton and CreateInvoiceForm).
7. Both "Copy contract link" and "Download contract" route to working endpoints — `/c/[id]` for the link, `/api/contracts/[id]/pdf` for the download.
