# PAY-7: Define a fee collection mechanism for fees not attached to a live customer payment

**Module:** payments  
**Type:** Decision

AMBIGUOUS: Decision required on which fee collection mechanism to use; requires data from detection analysis first

---

## Problem

PAY-4 delivered fee collection at source: the motko fee rides `application_fee_amount` on a destination charge when the customer pays. This works only when there is a live customer payment to attach to. Motko currently has no mechanism for collecting fees that are owed but have no charge to ride on. This blocks three existing cards that assume the ability to recover fees retroactively, and leaves any future fee correction with no implementation path. The system's stated policy is to correct legacy money records retroactively rather than fix-forward, but that policy is currently unimplementable.

## Scope: blocked on decision

This card cannot proceed to implementation until a decision is made on which collection mechanism to use. Four options exist:

1. **One-off Stripe invoice to the contractor** — cleanest, most visible, highest friction
2. **Charge against connected account balance** — fastest, least visible, highest risk to user trust
3. **Net against future application fees** — no new rail, invisible unless surfaced clearly
4. **Write off everything pre-cutover, go fix-forward only** — cheapest, reverses stated policy

The decision requires quantifying what is actually at stake:
- Total value of fees recoverable by the two backfills ([BACKFILL] Recover fees lost to partial settlePaidJob settlement, [BACKFILL] Recover fees lost to concurrent over-waiving of free jobs)
- Count of stranded pending collections with null provider ref and their value
- Count of contractors affected

This data does not exist yet. The detection halves of both backfills must run in dry-run mode first to produce it.

## Explicitly out of scope

- No change to the fee amount, banding, or the free-job allowance
- No change to fee-at-source on the normal path (PAY-4 remains as is)
- No customer-facing change of any kind — this is contractor-side only
- No implementation of any collection mechanism until the decision is made

## Files

Cannot be specified until the mechanism is chosen. Each of the four options would touch different files:
- Option 1 (Stripe invoice) would add to `src/lib/payments/` and likely create a new collection flow
- Option 2 (account balance debit) would interact with Stripe Connect balance operations
- Option 3 (net against future fees) would modify fee calculation in `src/lib/payments/compute-motko-fee.ts` or similar
- Option 4 (write off) would require no new implementation

## Edge cases

Blocked on mechanism decision. Each option has different edge cases:
- Invoicing: contractor no longer active, email bounces, invoice goes unpaid
- Balance debit: insufficient balance in connected account
- Netting: contractor stops using motko before debt clears
- Write-off: none (no collection = no edge cases)

## Risks

**Money system risk:** All four options involve deciding how motko collects money it says it is owed. Option 2 (balance debit) carries the highest risk of eroding user trust — taking money from a tradesperson's balance without fresh authorization. Option 1 (invoice) carries support risk — a surprise invoice months after a job is a support conversation regardless of amount. Option 4 (write-off) reverses stated policy but eliminates all collection risk.

**Data dependency:** The decision cannot be made rationally without knowing the total value at stake. If the two backfills and stranded collections total £50, option 4 is obviously correct. If they total £5,000, the decision is different.

**Blocking three cards:** This decision is currently blocking:
- [BACKFILL] Recover fees lost to partial settlePaidJob settlement
- [BACKFILL] Recover fees lost to concurrent over-waiving of free jobs  
- Add a reaper for stranded pending fee collections with null provider ref

## DECISION NEEDED

**Question:** Which mechanism should motko use to collect fees owed when there is no live customer payment to attach to?

**Options:**  
(a) One-off Stripe invoice to the contractor  
(b) Charge against the connected account balance (transfer reversal or debit)  
(c) Net against future application fees (increase fee on next N jobs until debt clears)  
(d) Write off everything pre-cutover and go fix-forward only  

**Recommendation:** Cannot recommend without the numbers. Run detection first: if the total value is small (order of £50–200), option (d) write-off unblocks three cards immediately and eliminates all collection risk and support burden. If the total is material (order of £1,000+), options (a) or (c) are viable, with (a) being cleanest and most auditable despite higher friction.

**Required first step:** Run the detection halves of both backfills in dry-run mode to produce:
- Total GBP value recoverable from partial settlement gaps
- Total GBP value recoverable from over-waived free jobs
- Count and value of stranded pending collections with null provider ref
- Count of distinct contractors affected across all three categories
