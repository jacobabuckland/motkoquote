# REF-3: Five activations credit one banked month

**Source:** [Notion roadmap item](https://app.notion.com/p/REF-3-Five-activations-credit-one-banked-month-3d21e4f908b48103a3afe0ee25436903)

**Module:** app

**Order:** 32 of 34

## Problem

"Free until further notice" is an uncountable liability. Discrete months are a countable number, and the liability must be visible. Five activated referrals now credit one banked subscription month. These credits are stored as discrete records with a consumed/unconsumed state and applied one at a time to Stripe subscription invoices for the £9.99/month plan.

"Invoice" here means the Stripe subscription invoice, not a row in the `invoices` table. The `invoices` table holds the trade's bill to their customer; a referral reward earned by the trade can never come out of their customer's bill.

## Scope

### What changes

1. **Banking logic in paid-job settlement:** `planPaidJobSettlement` in `src/lib/paid-job-settlement.ts` already recognises activations via `facts.activatedReferralCount` (the count AFTER incrementing). Every fifth activation (5, 10, 15, ...) now emits a month-credit record to be persisted alongside the existing `referral_unlock` ledger entry.

2. **Consumption path:** A new module `src/lib/referral-credits.ts` provides the consumption function that atomically claims one unconsumed month credit when a Stripe subscription invoice is being paid. The claim is conditional—it can only match an unconsumed row—so two concurrent attempts against the same credit result in exactly one consumption.

3. **Storage:** Migration `00000000000075_referral_credits.sql` creates the `referral_credits` table with:
   - Discrete month records with `consumed` boolean state
   - Standard RLS policy: `contractor_id in (select id from contractors where owner_user_id = auth.uid())`
   - No `insert`/`update`/`delete` grant to `anon` or `authenticated` (service-role only)
   - Applied manually by Jacob before this PR merges (schema precedes code)

## Explicitly out of scope

- What happens to unconsumed months at cancellation (SUB-6)
- Anything about the fee schedule or the £9.99 price itself
- Changes to the existing free-job grant behaviour (both rewards fire on the fifth activation)

## Files

- `src/lib/paid-job-settlement.ts` (modify) — emit month-credit record on every fifth activation
- `src/lib/referral-credits.ts` (new) — consumption path: atomically claim one unconsumed month
- `supabase/migrations/00000000000075_referral_credits.sql` (new) — discrete month records table with RLS
- `tests/acceptance/660.test.ts` (new) — acceptance tests

## Edge cases

### Activation thresholds
- Four activations bank nothing; the fifth banks exactly one month
- The tenth activation banks the second month (not two months on the tenth)
- Each multiple of five banks exactly one additional month (15th → third, 20th → fourth, etc.)

### Concurrent consumption
- Two concurrent consumption attempts against the same unconsumed credit result in exactly one succeeding
- The conditional claim matches `consumed = false` with a row-level lock or equivalent atomicity guarantee
- The second attempt finds no matching unconsumed row and returns null/nothing

### Multiple invoices
- Ten activations bank two months total
- These are consumed sequentially: first invoice consumes first month, second invoice consumes second month
- Never both months consumed against one invoice (which would produce £0 on one, full price on the next)

### No cap on banked months
- Unlike free jobs which cap at `MAX_BANKED_FREE_JOBS = 10`, referral month credits have no maximum
- A contractor may accumulate unlimited months via ongoing referral activations
- This is deliberate per the problem statement: the liability is visible as a number, but uncapped by decision D26

### Banking does not replace free-job grant
- The existing `referral_unlock` ledger entry still fires on every fifth activation (granting +5 free jobs)
- Month banking happens **in addition to** the free-job grant, not instead of it
- Both rewards are earned on the same activation event

## Risks

### Money
This affects what contractors are charged for the £9.99/month subscription. The consumption logic must be tested against a stubbed client to verify the atomic claim shape before any live Stripe integration touches it.

### Migration timing
Schema must precede code. Migration 75 must be applied to production manually via `supabase db push` before this PR merges. If the code deploys before the migration runs, any activation attempt will break on the missing `referral_credits` table.

### Stripe subscription invoice integration
This spec describes the banking and consumption **data model**. The actual integration with Stripe subscription invoices—reading the upcoming invoice, applying the credit, reducing the amount—is out of scope here and belongs to a future item. The consumption function signature and atomicity guarantees are designed for that integration.

## Derived criteria

I understood the human to be asking for:

1. Every fifth referral activation (5th, 10th, 15th, ...) banks exactly one subscription month credit
2. The banking happens in `planPaidJobSettlement`, the single place activations are recognised
3. Credits are stored as discrete records with a `consumed` boolean field
4. A consumption function atomically claims one unconsumed credit via a conditional update
5. Ten activations result in two discrete credits that are consumed across two separate subscription invoices
6. Concurrent consumption attempts against the same credit result in exactly one succeeding
7. No cap on how many months can be banked (differs from the 10-job cap on free jobs)
8. Banking a month does not replace the existing free-job grant—both fire on the fifth activation
9. The settlement logic is testable by calling `planPaidJobSettlement` with constructed facts (no client required)
10. The consumption logic is testable with a stubbed Supabase client asserting the conditional update query shape
