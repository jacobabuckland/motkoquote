# Issue #152: Build the reassurance strip on the customer payment page

## Problem

The customer payment page (`i/[id]/page.tsx:120`) currently presents a single text-xs text-muted sentence as the entire reassurance to a customer about to transfer a large sum by bank. This is the highest-stakes moment on any Motko surface — the customer does not have a relationship with Motko, only with the trade, and is being asked to authenticate with their bank on a page they have never seen. The single sentence carries insufficient design weight for this critical moment.

## Scope: what changes

Replace the single reassurance sentence (lines 122-126 in `i/[id]/page.tsx`) with a reassurance strip component that renders:

- A shield glyph (SVG icon)
- The payee company name in a bold weight
- Two approved regulatory copy strings (see below), rendered verbatim
- The approved copy strings must be held in a separate module (`src/lib/payment-reassurance-copy.ts`) so they can be changed without touching layout code

Update the pay button to include the amount in its label: "Pay £8,132 by bank" instead of just "Pay by bank". The amount must use identical formatting to the amount displayed elsewhere on the page (via `formatGBP`).

All strip text must meet AA contrast requirements. No element in the strip may use `text-muted` at `text-xs` size. The strip must render above the fold on a 375px-wide viewport.

**Approved copy strings (use verbatim):**

1. "Payments are processed by TrueLayer, authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority."
2. "Your money goes straight from your bank to {trade}."

The `{trade}` placeholder is replaced at render time with the trade's company name as already displayed on the page.

## Explicitly out of scope: what does not change

- Do not author, edit, paraphrase, or improve the copy. Use the supplied strings verbatim.
- Do not change payment initiation, the TrueLayer flow, or any payment logic.
- Do not change how the amount is calculated or retrieved from the database.
- Do not add trust badges, third-party logos, or testimonials beyond the supplied copy.
- Do not change the page's data fetching or database queries.
- Do not change any other text on the page.

## Files

The Engineer is expected to touch:

- `src/app/i/[id]/page.tsx` — replace lines 122-126 (the current reassurance text) with the new reassurance strip component
- `src/app/i/[id]/pay-button.tsx` — update button label to include the formatted amount
- `src/components/ui/reassurance-strip.tsx` (new) — the reassurance strip component
- `src/lib/payment-reassurance-copy.ts` (new) — module exporting the approved copy strings

## Edge cases

**Payee company name is missing:** Render the strip without the name rather than an empty bold element. The copy that depends on the name ("Your money goes straight from your bank to {trade}") should either omit the trade name or render the sentence without the bold element, but must not show an empty bold or "{trade}" literal.

**Amount is null:** Render the button label without the amount ("Pay by bank") rather than "Pay £0 by bank". Report this case in development; it should not occur in production since invoices always have an amount.

**Copy strings are not supplied:** Stop and report. Do not proceed with placeholder text. The copy module must export the strings; if the import fails or the strings are undefined, this is a build-time error.

**Very long company name:** Truncate with an ellipsis at the strip's bounds. Do not wrap to a third line or break the layout on narrow viewports.

**Legacy persisted data:** Invoices created before this change render the strip identically. No stored fields change; all data required already exists on the invoice record.

**User-visible:** Yes, to the trade's customers, on the highest-stakes page in the product (the payment page). This is a public-facing page with no authentication.

## Risks

⚠️ **Legal sign-off is outstanding.** The copy strings provided are approved for build but NOT approved for production. They are with a lawyer now. The PR implementing this item must NOT merge to `main` until legal sign-off is confirmed in writing on the PR. Build it, preview it, review it — do not merge it.

**Regulatory constraint:** TrueLayer is the authorised institution. Motko is not. TrueLayer is authorised and regulated by the FCA under the Payment Services Regulations 2017 as a Payment Initiation Service Provider; motko holds no such authorisation. Copy on this page must attribute authorisation to TrueLayer and must never state or imply that motko is FCA regulated. The approved copy satisfies this constraint; do not deviate from it.

**Sequencing risk:** This item touches the same route (`i/[id]/page.tsx`) as the capability-token security work. Sequence that work first, or batch these items together to avoid merge conflicts.

**High-stakes flow:** This is the payment page where customers transfer thousands of pounds. Test the component thoroughly on multiple viewport sizes (especially 375px width), verify the copy renders exactly as supplied, and confirm the amount in the button matches the invoice amount exactly.
