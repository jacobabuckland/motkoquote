How the fee is charged

  • The fee is charged on the job value excluding VAT. If you are VAT registered, the VAT you collect for HMRC is not part of what motko charges on.
  • The fee is charged per payment. A job paid in stages is charged on each stage, not once on the whole job.
  • The fee is taken when your customer pays. We deduct it from their payment before it reaches you.
  • Nothing is charged until you have been paid. If the customer does not pay, you do not pay either.
  • The fee is worked out when your customer pays, not when you send the quote. A quote you sent before a price change is charged at the price in force on the day it is paid.

VAT

motko is not currently registered for VAT, so there is no VAT in the fees above and none is added to them. The figures on this page are what you pay.

When motko registers for VAT, VAT will be added to the fee, and we will say so before it takes effect.

How free jobs work

A free job credit is applied to one payment, not to a whole job. If a job is paid in stages, the credit covers one stage and the remaining stages are charged normally.

A free job covers the whole motko fee on that payment, whatever the job is worth. There is nothing to pay.
