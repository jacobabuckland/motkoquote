Pricing

Simple fees, only charged when you get paid

Your first three jobs are free

Get started with no commitment. Your first three jobs cost nothing — no trial period, no credit card required.

Fee structure

After your free jobs, we charge a small fee per job based on the job value:

Up to £1,000
£2 per job
£1,001 to £3,000
£6 per job
Above £3,000
£10 per job

All fees are VAT-inclusive. VAT is included in the fee shown — we never add it on top.

The fee is taken when your customer pays. We deduct it from their payment before it reaches you.

Nothing is charged until you've been paid. If the customer doesn't pay, you don't pay either.

Earn free jobs by referring others

Know another tradesperson who could use motko? Refer them and earn free jobs when they complete their first paid job.

Standard tier

3 free jobs per referral who completes their first paid job

Premium tier

After 5 referrals, earn 5 free jobs per referral (up from 3)

Free job credits stack and accumulate — they never expire.

How free jobs work

A free job covers the standard £2 fee. If you use a free job on a higher-value job (where the fee would be £6 or £10), you pay the difference above £2.

Job up to £1,000

Standard fee: £2
With free job credit: £0

Job £1,001 to £3,000

Standard fee: £6
With free job credit: £6 - £2 = you pay the difference above £2

Job above £3,000

Standard fee: £10
With free job credit: £10 - £2 = you pay the difference above £2
