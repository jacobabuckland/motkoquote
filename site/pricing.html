Pricing

Two charges, stated separately, and only when you get paid

Your first three jobs are free

Get started with no commitment. The motko service fee is waived on your first three payments — no trial period, no credit card required. Payment processing still applies; the rules are set out below.

What motko costs

Two things are charged on a job, and they are separate. One is the bank's cost of moving the money. The other is what motko charges for the work of getting you there.

Payment processing

What the payment rail costs, passed through at cost, capped at £5.00 per payment. motko does not mark this up and does not keep any part of it.

motko service fee

A percentage of the job, charged in bands. Each band applies only to the part of the job that falls inside it.

How the fee is charged

  • The fee is charged on the job value excluding VAT. If you are VAT registered, the VAT you collect for HMRC is not part of what motko charges on.
  • The fee is charged per payment. A job paid in stages is charged on each stage, not once on the whole job.
  • The fee is taken when your customer pays. We deduct it from their payment before it reaches you.
  • Nothing is charged until you have been paid. If the customer does not pay, you do not pay either.
  • The fee is worked out when your customer pays, not when you send the quote. A quote you sent before a price change is charged at the price in force on the day it is paid.

VAT

motko is not currently registered for VAT, so there is no VAT in the fees above and none is added to them. The figures on this page are what you pay.

When motko registers for VAT, two things change and we will say so before they do: VAT will be added to the service fee, and the payment processing pass-through will be quoted at cost plus VAT rather than absorbed.

How free jobs work

A free job credit is applied to one payment, not to a whole job. If a job is paid in stages, the credit covers one stage and the remaining stages are charged normally.

A free job covers the standard £2.00 service fee. On a larger job, where the service fee is more than that, you pay the difference above £2.00.

Payment processing is charged on a free job as normal. A free job means no service fee to pay, not no cost to move the money.
